Rule 1: Quote & Analyze Job Description
◦ Extract key phrases using blockquotes (>)
◦ Focus on "what you'll do" and qualifications you meet

Rule 2: Respond Directly to Requirements
◦ Address each quoted requirement with specific experience
◦ Use exact terminology from job description
◦ Include measurable outcomes and links to relevant work

Rule 3: Tell Stories for Key Qualifications
◦ Select 2-3 most important matches
◦ Connect to themes like Leadership, Initiative, Problem-Solving

Rule 4: Integrate Company Research
◦ Quote company mission/values
◦ Reference recent company news or initiatives
◦ Mention personal experience with their products if applicable

Rule 5: Structure in Markdown
◦ Concise intro → Quoted requirements with responses → Brief conclusion
◦ Format for clean PDF conversion

Rule 6: Be Authentic & Specific
◦ Use your voice with technical details
◦ Reference real projects and solutions

Rule 7: Optimize for Humans & ATS
◦ Include keywords naturally
◦ Keep to one page with clear headings
