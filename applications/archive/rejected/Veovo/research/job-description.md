## Job Description

Veovo is a market leader in Airport Smart Technology. With a global footprint of customers and capabilities that cover Airport Operations, Revenue management and Passenger Flow. Some of the world’s largest airports rely on Veovo. Focused on driving airport performance, our solutions enable airports to handle more aircraft and more passengers, with less delays and less queues. Our platforms are built on a modern tech stack, often delivered from the cloud as SaaS and leveraging intelligence to enable our customer to “go brilliantly”.

Who we are looking for:
- Potential over Pedigree: Whether you have 1 year of experience or 10, we care about your ability to learn, solve complex problems, and your drive to build great software.
- Full Stack Mindset: Your heart likely beats for the Backend (.NET Core), but you aren't afraid to jump into the Frontend (Angular) to build cohesive features.
- AI-First Mindset: We want people who aren't afraid of AI. We expect you to use modern tools (LLMs, Copilots) to accelerate your work, while maintaining the critical thinking to know when the AI is wrong.
- Office Culture & Flexibility: We start with 100% office-based onboarding, then transition to a Hybrid model (3 days in the office, 2 days remote).

In this role, you will:
- Develop and maintain software as part of a self-organising, multi-disciplined scrum team.
- Take ownership of features end-to-end: primarily backend logic but handling the necessary UI work in Angular to bring your features to life.
- Work with a modern tech stack: .NET Core, Angular, Kubernetes, AWS, and more.

What you’ll need:
- Solid foundations in C# / .NET.
- The ability (or strong willingness to learn) to handle simple to mid-level tasks in Angular.
- High Agency: proactive, asks "why," and doesn't wait for permission to improve things.
- Good communication in English.
- A degree in Computer Science/Software Engineering is great, but proven skill and hacker spirit matter just as much.

What we offer:
- Cutting-edge technologies in a small and ambitious team in a flat company structure.
- Rapid growth opportunities and structured professional training.
- Hybrid flexibility: 3/2 hybrid split after onboarding.
- Exciting and innovative projects in the aviation industry.
