About us

PHQ (phq.nz) is an independent, digitally led creative agency based in Auckland, founded by the Phantom London studio (http://www.phantom.land/). Our very essence is to shape shift - to adapt and evolve, collaborating with brands that challenge and endlessly inspire us. We transform challenges of all shapes and sizes into inventive, engaging and performance driven solutions. For us, every project is a unique opportunity to lead the industry and deliver groundbreaking results. Put simply, we want to do incredible work that we’re proud of and make our clients love us. Where there’s an innovative solution to be had or simple amends to complete, we will tackle it even better than anyone could have imagined.

About the role

As an Junior Frontend Developer you will collaborate with other Developers, Designers and Producers on technical solutions that align closely with a creative vision (web applications, native apps, mobile experiences, cloud architecture, digital and social advertising as well as experimental works)

Your primary focus will be to develop production-ready code that meet UI/UX web accessibility and browser compatibility.

About you

You are ready to move your career to the next level and build on your foundational knowledge of Angular and Typescript. You’re somebody who’s excited by crafting beautiful & clean user experiences and gets a kick out of solving complex problems and you constantly look for ways to push the boundaries of the features you work on.

You'll need:

- 1-2 years development experience
- Knowledge of relevant programming languages and methodologies (Typescript, HTML5, CSS3)
- Knowledge of Javascript frameworks (e.g. Angular or React)
- Excellent written and spoken English
- Confident working with version control (Git).
- Knowledge of Three.js, D3.js or other Creative technologies is a plus
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Capability to work on simultaneous projects and meet deadlines
- Experience giving and receiving code reviews

Bonus Experience:

- Strong coding fundamentals - we love using the latest technologies so you should be eager and willing to learn new frameworks/languages and apply them to day-day work when required.
- Amazing examples of past work in web applications.
- Self-motivated with good time management.
