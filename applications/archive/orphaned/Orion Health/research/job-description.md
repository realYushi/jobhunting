Shape the Future of Global Healthcare Technology
At Orion Health, we're on a mission to make healthcare smarter, safer, and more connected - for clinicians, patients, and the wider community.
Our software underpins digital health ecosystems worldwide, empowering healthcare professionals with timely, accurate information while enabling patients to take an active role in managing their wellbeing. From the Amadeus Digital Care Record, which gives clinicians a complete and unified view of each patient's health journey, to the Virtuoso Digital Front Door, which helps individuals engage directly with their care, we're redefining how healthcare is delivered and experienced.
With solutions deployed across APAC, North America, and EMEA, our technology supports the delivery of care to millions globally.
What You Can Expect
Join a collaborative, agile team working on innovative products that improve patient outcomes and empower people to take control of their health. Depending on your skills and interests, you may be involved in:
Designing and developing robust, scalable software solutions across our product suite.
Working across the full stack - from modern front-end technologies (React, JavaScript, HTML, CSS) to enterprise-grade back-end systems (Java, SQL, cloud).
Leveraging cloud platforms like AWS to deliver secure, high-performing healthcare solutions.
Collaborating with diverse teams across engineering, product, and design to bring ideas to life.
Helping shape best practices, improve development processes, and deliver high-quality, maintainable code
