# Company Profile: Kitea Health

## Basic Information
- **Company Name**: Kitea Health Ltd
- **Industry**: Medical Technology / Healthcare / Medical Devices
- **Size**: Small startup (~15-20 employees based on leadership structure)
- **Founded**: Spun out from University of Auckland research
- **Headquarters**: Level 6, 70 Symonds Street, Grafton, Auckland 1010, New Zealand
- **US Office**: 2140 South Dupont Hwy, Camden, 19934 Delaware, USA
- **Website**: https://kiteahealth.com

## Mission & Values
**Mission**: "Transforming healthcare for people living with chronic conditions" through next-generation micro-implantable devices for managing chronic health conditions.

**Core Values**: 
- **Patient-Centered Innovation**: Focus on improving outcomes for patients with heart failure and hydrocephalus
- **Scientific Excellence**: Grounded in rigorous research and clinical validation
- **Early Intervention**: Shifting from reactive to proactive healthcare management
- **Pressure-Guided Care**: Enabling informed medical decisions based on real pressure values
- **Global Health Impact**: Reducing healthcare inequity and improving access to monitoring

## Business Model
**Product**: Kitea System - world's most accurate implantable sensor for pressure monitoring
- **Primary Markets**: Heart failure (26 million people worldwide) and hydrocephalus (1 million in US)
- **Technology**: Micro-implantable devices that enable pressure monitoring at home and in hospital
- **Revenue Model**: Medical device sales, likely recurring monitoring services
- **Market Impact**: 
  - Heart failure: $108 billion annual global economic burden
  - Hydrocephalus: $2 billion annual US healthcare costs
  - Estimated 30-62% reduction in healthcare costs through early intervention

## Key Technologies
**Core Technology**: Micro-implantable pressure sensors with wireless telemetry
- **Applications**: Heart failure monitoring, hydrocephalus (brain pressure) monitoring
- **Capabilities**: Real-time pressure measurements, home and hospital monitoring
- **Integration**: Data management systems, physician portals, threshold notifications
- **Manufacturing**: In-house prototyping with 3D printing, external supplier partnerships

**Development Focus**:
- AI-based predictive modeling for brain pressure signals
- Database architecture (RDBMS/NoSQL)
- Custom user interfaces and commercial software integration
- Medical device design controls and quality systems
