# Job Description: Software Engineering Summer Internship at Kitea Health

## Position Overview
- **Title**: Software Engineering Summer Intern
- **Department**: Engineering/Technology
- **Location**: Parnell, Auckland, New Zealand  
- **Type**: 10-week paid summer internship
- **Start Date**: October/November 2025
- **Application Deadline**: September 15, 2025

## Responsibilities

### Core Projects
- **Data Management Structure Design**: Contribute to the design and implementation of comprehensive data management architecture
- **Database Architecture**: Work on both RDBMS and NoSQL database systems to support medical device data collection and analysis
- **User Interface Development**: Potential involvement in custom-made user interface design or integration with commercial software tools
- **Technical Documentation**: Create documentation of the data management strategy following medical device development standards
- **Design Controls**: Learn and implement design controls required for medical device development in regulated environment

### Learning Opportunities
- Collaborate with cross-functional team including Data Science, Quality/Regulatory, and Mechatronics teams
- Gain hands-on experience in medical technology company environment
- Understand FDA regulatory requirements and medical device development lifecycle
- Work with real-world clinical data from ongoing first-in-human trials
- Exposure to AI/ML projects analyzing brain pressure signals for predictive modeling

## Requirements

### Must-Have
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or related field
- **Eligibility**: Must be studying at New Zealand tertiary education institution (NZQA Level 6-10) OR completed studies within last 12 months
- **Work Authorization**: Legal permission to work in New Zealand
- **Technical Foundation**: Understanding of database systems, software development principles
- **Academic Standing**: University transcript (official or unofficial) demonstrating good academic performance

### Preferred
- **Database Experience**: Practical experience with both RDBMS (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB, Redis) systems
- **Full-Stack Development**: Experience with modern web development frameworks and APIs
- **Healthcare/Medical Technology**: Interest or experience in healthcare technology, clinical data management
- **Documentation Skills**: Technical writing and documentation experience
- **UI/UX Design**: User interface design skills, particularly for complex technical applications

### Nice-to-Have
- **Regulated Environment Experience**: Understanding of quality systems, compliance, or regulatory requirements
- **Data Visualization**: Experience with data dashboard and visualization tools
- **Medical Device Knowledge**: Understanding of medical device development process
- **Academic Research**: Experience with research projects or clinical data analysis
- **Cross-functional Collaboration**: Experience working with clinical, quality, or regulatory teams
