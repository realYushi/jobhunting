# Orion Health - Culture & Values Analysis

## Core Values & Company Culture

### Company Values Framework
Orion Health's culture is built around their mission to "reimagine the healthcare experience for all" with three core pillars:

1. **EMPOWER** - People-centered approach
2. **REVEAL** - Data-driven insights
3. **UNIFY** - Collaborative healthcare ecosystems

## Workplace Culture

### "Community of Start-ups" Philosophy
- **Autonomous Product Teams**: Each product team has their own voice and autonomy
- **Entrepreneurial Spirit**: Individual teams can decide how they operate and contribute
- **Innovation Focus**: Encouraging creative problem-solving and independent thinking
- **Mission-Driven**: All teams aligned toward improving health outcomes globally

### Employee Experience Values

#### **1. Global Mindset & Mobility**
- **TravelFlex**: 30 days per year working from anywhere globally
- **Global Mobility Program**: Encouraged movement between 17 offices across 11 countries
- **International Collaboration**: Working with colleagues worldwide on shared mission
- **Cultural Diversity**: "No matter your background, where you come from or what you look like, you matter at Orion Health"

#### **2. Work-Life Integration**
- **Flexible Working Arrangements**: Design your workday to suit your lifestyle
- **LeavePlus**: Extra leave on top of standard annual leave entitlement
- **Summer Fridays**: Additional time off during summer months
- **Remote/Hybrid Options**: Office and work-from-home flexibility

#### **3. Innovation & Technology**
- **Cutting-Edge Technology**: Latest languages, platforms, and tools
- **Modern Tech Stack**: Apache Kafka, Spark, Elasticsearch, Kubernetes, React, Java, Scala
- **Machine Learning Focus**: AI and ML integration in healthcare solutions
- **Cloud-First Approach**: AWS-based development and deployment

#### **4. Learning & Growth**
- **Unlimited LinkedIn Learning**: Access to professional development
- **Technology Expertise**: Working with most talented and experienced tech workers
- **Healthcare Domain Knowledge**: Opportunity to become expert in digital health
- **Career Mobility**: Internal movement and progression opportunities

#### **5. Well-being & Support**
- **Health/Medical Insurance**: Comprehensive coverage (eligibility applies)
- **Mental Health Support**: Focus on employee well-being
- **Community Impact**: Opportunities for volunteer work and community service
- **Inclusive Environment**: Commitment to belonging and being yourself
