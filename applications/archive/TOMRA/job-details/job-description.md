# TOMRA Software Engineer Summer Intern - Job Description

## Position Details
- **Company**: TOMRA Food
- **Position**: Software Engineer Summer Intern
- **Location**: Auckland, New Zealand
- **Application Deadline**: August 21st, 2025
- **Duration**: Summer internship program

## Company Overview
TOMRA Food is a multinational company specializing in sensor-based sorting and food processing solutions. With over 50 years in business, TOMRA operates under the mission "Every Resource Counts™" focusing on food safety and minimizing food loss. The company values innovation, responsibility, and passion in their approach to automation and sustainability.

## Role Description
The Software Engineer Summer Intern position focuses on software development with emphasis on automation and streamlining service request workflows. The role involves designing, developing, and maintaining code, scripts, or tools that reduce manual intervention and improve response times.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Software Development**: Main duties centered around software development
- **Automation Focus**: Design, develop, and maintain code, scripts, or tools that reduce manual intervention
- **Service Workflow Optimization**: Improve response times and streamline service request workflows
- **Cross-functional Collaboration**: Work with teams across different departments
- **System Integration**: Focus on integrating various systems and services
- **Service Delivery Enhancement**: Contribute to improving overall service delivery efficiency
