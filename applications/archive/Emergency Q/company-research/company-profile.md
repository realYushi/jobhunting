## Business Model & Technology Platform

### Core Product Suite
Emergency Q operates a comprehensive healthcare technology platform consisting of three integrated solutions:

#### 1. **Patient Mobile Application**
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android
- **Features:** 
  - Real-time ED and urgent care wait times
  - Interactive map of nearby healthcare facilities
  - Pricing information specific to patients
  - COVID-19 information directory
  - Condition assessment guidance (ED vs. urgent care)

#### 2. **Hospital Emergency Department Portal**
- **Target Users:** Frontline ED staff, triage teams
- **Features:**
  - Desktop portal for real-time clinic integration
  - Digital waiting room displays (multilingual support)
  - Patient transfer capabilities to primary care
  - Workflow integration for busy ED environments

#### 3. **Ambulance Service Integration**
- **Purpose:** Reduce non-emergency ambulance callouts
- **Features:**
  - Call centre integration tools
  - Safe transfer protocols for non-acute cases
  - COVID-19 exposure risk reduction
  - Cost savings optimization
