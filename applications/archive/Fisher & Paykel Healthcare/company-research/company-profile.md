# Fisher & Paykel Healthcare - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded**: 1969  
**Industry**: Medical Device Manufacturing - Respiratory & Sleep Apnea Care  
**Location**: Auckland, New Zealand (Headquarters: 15 Maurice Paykel Place, East Tamaki)  
**Global Reach**: Products sold in 120+ countries worldwide  
**Stock Exchange**: Listed on NZX and ASX

## Business Profile

Fisher & Paykel Healthcare is a world leader in the design, manufacture and marketing of medical devices for:

-   **Acute and Chronic Respiratory Care**: Humidification systems, nasal high flow therapy
-   **Sleep Apnea Treatment**: CPAP masks and devices
-   **Surgical Applications**: Surgical humidification systems
-   **Anesthesia**: Optiflow THRIVE technology

## Recent Financial Performance (2024-2025)

-   **Strong Growth**: 18% revenue growth in H1 FY25
-   **Revenue Guidance**: $1.9-2.0 billion for FY25
-   **Profit Growth**: 44% increase in net profit after tax H1 FY25
-   **Market Position**: Consistent revenue growth across all product groups
