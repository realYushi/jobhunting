# Fisher & Paykel Healthcare - Informatics Internship 2025-2026

## Programme Details

-   **Duration**: 24 November 2025 to 20 February 2026
-   **Team**: Informatics
-   **Application Deadline**: Sunday 3 August 2025
-   **Contact**: intern@fphcare.co.nz

## Programme Overview

Our internship programme offers mentorship, training, and social activities to help you build professional skills and connections. In our hands-on, proactive environment, you'll be working with the latest technology turning your thinking into reality.

With roles available in our Informatics team, you can learn from the best! We work collaboratively to enable practical learning in our open-plan work space.

## About You

You are a 3rd or 4th year Engineering/Science student specialising in Software Engineering / Computer Systems / Computer Science.

You're passionate about software engineering or science and your CV demonstrates this through your work experience, hobbies and university projects.

## About Us

Fisher & Paykel is an iconic New Zealand brand and a world leader in the design, manufacture and marketing of medical devices. We aim to foster an inclusive work environment founded on our culture of care and collaboration. Here you'll have the trust and support to ask questions, generate ideas and do your best work.

Important to this culture are our employee-led networks, formed around shared identities and experiences, these include:

-   **Spectra**: focused on rainbow inclusion
-   **Manaaki**: focused on Māori language and culture
-   **WISE**: focused on women in science and engineering
-   **ReThink**: focused on neurodiversity

We also commit to creating a positive lasting impact on society, shown by our support of New Zealand organisations such as Te Uru Tāngata - Centre for Workplace Inclusion, Pride Pledge, and Global Women.
