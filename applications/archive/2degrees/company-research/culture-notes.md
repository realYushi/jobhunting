# Culture Notes: 2degrees

## Core Cultural Foundation

### Fighting for Fair Philosophy
- **Central Mission**: "Fighting for Fair" drives every business decision and cultural practice
- **Customer-First Approach**: Genuine commitment to customer fairness over profit maximization
- **Industry Disruption**: Proud history of challenging unfair telecommunications practices
- **Value-Driven Culture**: Values guide behavior rather than rigid corporate rules

### Challenger Mentality (Company DNA)
- **Historical Context**: Built to break up the telecommunications duopoly in New Zealand
- **Innovation Leadership**: First to introduce Carryover Data, Data Clock, unlimited plans
- **Status Quo Disruption**: Actively encourage employees to think differently and challenge conventional approaches
- **Market Impact**: Created the "2degrees effect" - recognized industry disruption

## Work Environment & Culture

### Employee-Centric Approach
- **Authentic Care**: "We're humans too" - recognition of work-life integration needs
- **Flexibility First**: Work arrangements designed around outcomes, not hours
- **Individual Recognition**: Celebrate unique contributions and different perspectives
- **Growth-Oriented**: Strong focus on personal and professional development

### Inclusive & Diverse Workplace
- **Rainbow Tick Certified**: Official recognition for LGBTQ+ inclusive practices
- **Cultural Celebration**: Active employee groups for various backgrounds and interests
- **Different Perspectives Valued**: "To be different, we need diverse people and different ways of thinking"
- **No Fitting In Required**: "We'll never ask you to fit-in; we'll encourage you to stretch-out"

### Team Dynamics
- **Collaborative Environment**: Cross-functional teamwork emphasized
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Culture of helping colleagues and sharing expertise
- **Open Communication**: Transparent communication across all levels
- **Mutual Support**: Strong sense of collective purpose and mutual assistance

## Values in Action

### 🧡 Be Customer Led
- **Customer Impact Focus**: Decisions evaluated based on customer benefit
- **External Perspective**: Regular consideration of customer experience in planning
- **Service Excellence**: Commitment to exceeding customer expectations
- **Fair Treatment**: Ensuring customer fairness in all interactions

### 💚 Care
- **Genuine Concern**: Authentic care for colleagues, customers, and community
- **Wellbeing Focus**: Strong emphasis on mental health and employee wellness
- **Community Connection**: Active participation in community initiatives
- **Environmental Responsibility**: Sustainability focus across operations

### 💛 Be Bold
- **Innovation Encouragement**: Safe environment for new ideas and experimentation
- **Risk-Taking Support**: Backing calculated risks and learning from failures
- **Challenge Authority**: Encouraged to question processes and suggest improvements
- **Industry Leadership**: Setting trends rather than following competitors

### 💙 Own It
- **Accountability Culture**: Taking responsibility for outcomes and decisions
- **Results-Driven**: Focus on delivering meaningful results
- **Problem-Solving**: Proactive approach to identifying and solving challenges
- **Personal Growth**: Taking ownership of individual development

## Benefits & Perks Culture

### Work-Life Integration
- **Flexible Working**: Remote work, flexible hours, outcome-focused approach
- **Summer Hours**: 2pm Friday finish during January-February
- **Long Weekends**: 12pm finish before public holidays
- **Fair Leave**: One additional personal day per year to use as desired

### Professional Development
- **Growth Conversations**: Regular career development discussions
- **Coaching Academy**: Internal coaching and mentorship programs
- **Mentor Walks**: Informal mentoring opportunities
- **Learning Platform**: Award-winning online training resources

### Family & Community Support
- **Fair Parenting**: 26 weeks at 100% salary for parental leave
- **Partner Support**: 2 weeks paid partner leave
- **Flexible Return**: Work 4 days, paid for 5 (3 months post-return)
- **Fair Days**: Paid volunteer time for community service

### Wellness & Health
- **Health Insurance**: Fully subsidized medical insurance including specialist cover
- **Employee Assistance**: 6 free counseling sessions through Telus
- **Wellness Programs**: Yoga, pilates, mindfulness workshops
- **Free Health Services**: Flu vaccinations, health talks
