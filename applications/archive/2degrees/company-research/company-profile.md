# Company Profile: 2degrees

## Company Overview

**Founded**: 2009  
**Headquarters**: Auckland, New Zealand  
**Industry**: Telecommunications  
**Position**: Third-largest telecommunications company in New Zealand  
**Status**: Privately owned challenger telco

## Business Scale & Operations

- **Staff**: 1,673 New Zealand employees (as of 2025)
- **Network**: 2,101 cell towers nationwide
- **Infrastructure**: 4,600km of fibre broadband underground
- **Customer Base**: Over 1 million New Zealanders
- **Market Position**: Third-largest telco in NZ, recognized as "fairest telco" by Canstar

## Core Business Segments

### Mobile Services
- Pay Monthly plans with innovative Carryover Data feature
- Prepay plans (12 times per year vs competitors' 13)
- Group plans and sidekick plans
- Student discounts and community benefits
- Unlimited Travel SIM and roaming services

### Broadband & Internet
- Fibre broadband plans
- Hyperfibre plans (high-speed options)
- Wireless 4G and 5G broadband
- Rural broadband solutions
- Starlink business services partnership

### Business Solutions
- Enterprise mobile and data services
- Cloud services and data centres
- Managed LAN/WiFi solutions
- SD-WAN and cloud connectivity
- Security services (DNS Protect, DDoS Protection)
- Microsoft Teams integration
- Mobile Device Management (MDM)

### Energy Services
- 2degrees Power - electricity services
- Integrated utility offerings

## Technology Infrastructure

### Network Technology
- 4G LTE nationwide coverage
- 5G network deployment in progress
- 3G shutdown planned for late 2025 to enhance 4G and support 5G growth
- WiFi calling capabilities
- Nationwide mobile network serving most populated areas

### Innovation Focus
- Data Clock app for flexible data purchasing
- Carryover Data technology (first in NZ market)
- Free Data Hour feature on select plans
- Hyperfibre high-speed internet technology
- Mobile hotspot capabilities included in plans

### Strategic Partnerships

- Major device partnerships: Samsung, Apple, OPPO
