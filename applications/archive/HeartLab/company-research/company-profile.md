# HeartLab Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 2018  
**Founder & CEO:** Will Hewitt  
**Headquarters:** 305/150 Karangahape Road, Auckland CBD, Auckland 1010, New Zealand  
**Industry:** Medical Technology (MedTech) - Cardiology Imaging  
**Company Stage:** Growth-stage SaaS company  
**Global Presence:** New Zealand, Australia, USA  

## Mission & Vision

**Mission:** To revolutionise cardiac diagnosis and empower cardiac clinicians to better address the growing cardiovascular disease crisis.

**Vision:** To create positive change for cardiology teams by significantly improving the cardiology imaging workflow with their cloud-based platform, ultimately benefiting patients through enhanced collaboration, remote work capabilities, and time-saving features.

## Business Model & Products

### Core Product: Cloud-Based Cardiology Imaging Platform
- **Primary Offering:** SaaS platform replacing legacy on-premise cardiology PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System) products
- **Technology:** Browser-based, cloud-native DICOM server
- **Pricing Model:** Monthly subscription + per-study fee (no per-user or per-machine charges)
- **Target Market:** Cardiology practices, cardiac imaging labs, and healthcare systems

### Key Features & Capabilities
1. **Multi-Modality Imaging Support:**
   - Echocardiograms
   - Stress echocardiograms
   - Vascular ultrasound
   - Nuclear cardiac testing

2. **Advanced Functionality:**
   - Near-instant image access and loading
   - Comprehensive post-processing package (2D and Doppler measurements)
   - Real-time collaboration capabilities
   - Customizable, branded report templates
   - Re-measurement capabilities without returning to imaging machines

3. **Integration Capabilities:**
   - DICOM connectivity
   - HL7 integration with PMS, CVIS/RIS, and EHR/EMR systems
   - DICOM worklists
   - Open APIs for flexible integration
   - AI partnerships (Us2.ai, Ultromics)

4. **Security & Compliance:**
   - SOC2 Type II certified
   - HIPAA compliant
   - Secure by Design principles
   - Enterprise-grade cloud security
