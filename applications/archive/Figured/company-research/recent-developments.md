# Figured Recent Developments & Strategic Initiatives

## 2025 Product Innovations

### **June 2025: Benchmarking Built for Advisory**
- **Launch:** New benchmarking feature integrated directly into Figured platform
- **Key Innovation:** Built from existing data - "No uploads, no formatting, just instant, relevant comparisons"
- **Target Users:** Leading advisory firms supporting dairy clients
- **Business Impact:** Transforms reporting moments into stronger conversations and deeper insights
- **Strategic Significance:** Enhances advisory relationships and data-driven decision making

### **March 2025: Milk Price Protection Launch**
- **Problem Addressed:** Milk price volatility management for NZ dairy farmers
- **Solution:** Proactive risk management tools for margin uncertainty
- **Market Need:** Dramatic seasonal price swings affecting farm financial management
- **Strategic Importance:** Addresses core financial stability concerns for dairy operations

### **February 2025: Auto Kill Sheet Processor**
- **AI Implementation:** AI-driven data extraction and processing
- **Efficiency Gain:** Reduces 20-minute task to 1 minute (95% time reduction)
- **Features:** Seamless invoice syncing, automated data extraction, quick approvals
- **Strategic Significance:** Demonstrates "meaningful AI integration" philosophy in action
