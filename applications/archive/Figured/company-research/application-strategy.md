# Figured Application Strategy & Strategic Positioning

## Executive Summary

Figured represents an exceptional opportunity for an AI-curious developer ready for meaningful agricultural technology impact. As an 11-year-old NZ-founded company supporting 35,000+ farming operations globally, Figured is at the intersection of essential industry innovation and cutting-edge AI implementation. Their philosophy of "meaningful AI integration, not AI sprinkles" aligns perfectly with production-ready development experience and eagerness for substantive AI work.
