## Who we are

Vista Group brings more people together to experience the magic of movies and cinema by providing a platform solution that connects the industry and powers the moviegoer experience. Vista Group is a New Zealand company with a global footprint, providing an innovative range of complementary solutions across the film industry, from production and distribution to cinema exhibition through to the experience of the moviegoer.

With more than 700 people across the globe, Vista Group says its people are its most important asset. The company prioritises diversity, inclusion, and an environment that empowers people to realise their full potential. Vista Group aims to do “good things with good people”.

## Role context
