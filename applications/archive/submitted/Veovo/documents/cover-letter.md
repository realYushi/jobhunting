I’m applying for the Full Stack Developer - Intermediate role at Veovo. What I can bring immediately is solid frontend experience from React and Vue, direct .NET experience, and a habit of taking ownership and learning unfamiliar parts of the stack when the feature needs it. I’d welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to Veovo’s team.
