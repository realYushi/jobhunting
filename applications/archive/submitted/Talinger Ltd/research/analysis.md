Our client is a leading digital connectivity business looking for a Full Stack Developer to help design, build, and maintain a suite of modern applications that support their global operations. You'll work with a modern tech stack, contribute to strategic engineering decisions, and help shape the future of their application environment. This role is primarily Auckland-based with hybrid flexibility. Sorry but this role will only be available for candidates with full rights to work in permanent employment in New Zealand.    If this feels like it might be a good fit, then please apply online asap. Thanks!
