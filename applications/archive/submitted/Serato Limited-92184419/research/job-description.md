# Junior Software Engineer C++ @ Serato Limited

Source: https://nz.seek.com/job/92184419?type=standard&ref=search-standalone&origin=cardTitle#sol=0022c0e5d00d507b1f8ee11d73c28fd9b257c3f3

Serato is one of the world leaders in audio software for professional DJs and musicians. World renowned artists such as Fatboy Slim, DJ Snake and Major Lazer love our products and perform with them on a regular basis.
Based in central Auckland, we have opportunities for a Junior software engineer to work on our C++ music production applications.  A software development role within Serato involves working in a small agile team to help shape and implement our core products.
To be successful in this role, you should be able to demonstrate:
Strong programming knowledge - ideally including C++Programming experience ideally in a real environmentA degree in computer science or a related field
Important soft skills:
A great communicator - oral and writtenAbility to establish and maintain cooperative and effective working relationships in a teamInitiative and the ability to work independently while also contributing effectively in a team
It would be great if you also have one or more of these:
Experience with or knowledge of multi-threaded or high performance programmingCross-platform application development with Windows and macOSExperience using 3rd party frameworks such as Qt, Boost, JuceUI developmentExperience with web application stack and programming languagesExperience with DSPExperience with backend db integration, SQL, RESTful Web Services Experience as a musician, producer or DJ
At Serato our culture is highly prized and so finding someone who will integrate well with the team and company, sharing our values of having fun whilst working hard and continually improving, is more important than ticking all the boxes. If you, love software and music and want to work in a high performing team at a company with a great culture this could be the role for you.
