# Software Engineer @ FirstCape Limited

Source: https://nz.seek.com/job/92492462?type=standard&ref=search-standalone&origin=cardTitle#sol=54dbd344dc7ed91aed20fa43ad2446a84da00689

About First Cape | Mo FirstCape 
FirstCape is one of New Zealand’s largest and best resourced wealth advisory and asset management business, creating an enhanced and broadened product offering to help our clients achieve their goals at whatever stage of their investment life cycle. Here at FirstCape, we believe that our people are our greatest asset. Our success is dependent on the collective talent, expertise, and dedication of each and every one of our team members. We are committed to creating an environment where everyone can thrive, grow, and contribute to their fullest potential.

At FirstCape, technology is central to how we deliver great client outcomes. We’re looking for a Software Engineer to join our Technology team and help build new digital products and client experiences on a modern, scalable platform. This is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys building new functionality within an established environment - balancing innovation, quality, and long-term sustainability.




About the opportunity | Mo te whai waahi
You’ll be part of a team focused on delivering a new client experience, working across web and mobile technologies to bring product ideas to life. This role blends hands-on engineering with product thinking - contributing to how features are designed, built and evolved over time. You’ll collaborate closely with Product, UX and platform teams, with real ownership of what you deliver.

Key Responsibilities | Ngā Kawenga Mahi


	Software engineering & code quality: Writing clean, maintainable, well-tested code aligned to engineering standards, with a strong focus on quality and reliability.
	Modern front-end development: Building intuitive, high-quality client experiences using React and modern JavaScript frameworks.
	Technical design & collaboration: Contributing to design discussions, code reviews and shaping how solutions are structured and delivered.
	Product development & evolution: Helping design and deliver new functionality, ensuring it integrates effectively with existing platforms and shared services.
	API integration & performance: Working with APIs and distributed systems, balancing usability, performance and scalability.
	Release, support & continuous improvement: Supporting the release process, resolving issues and proactively improving solutions over time.
	Security & sustainability: Embedding secure design principles and considering long-term maintainability in all solutions.
	Cross-team collaboration: Partnering with Product, UX and engineering teams to deliver high-quality outcomes and contribute to a positive team culture.


We work with a modern, evolving stack including:


	Frontend: React, React Query, Ant Design, React Native
	Testing & tooling: Vite, Vitest, Playwright, Mock Service Worker
	Platform: .NET APIs, ASP.N
