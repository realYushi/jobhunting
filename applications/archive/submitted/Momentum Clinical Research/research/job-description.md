# Junior Automation Engineer @ Momentum Clinical Research / PCRN

Source: https://nz.seek.com/job/91880862?token=1~3bb05ea0-de58-4b4c-8d24-618a1f839689&ref=post-apply-recs

Be part of the next generation of clinical research.

Momentum Clinical Research and PCRN operate the largest full-phase clinical trial network across Australia and New Zealand. With us, you’ll contribute to meaningful research that advances treatment options, in a team that values quality, collaboration and enjoying the work we do.

## The Opportunity

The role of Junior Automation Engineer plays an important part in how Momentum Clinical Research and PCRN deliver efficient, scalable clinical trial operations. Working on site in Takapuna, you will help streamline recruitment and data processes by building and maintaining automated workflows that connect systems and reduce manual effort. This is an opportunity to develop highly sought-after automation and AI skills while contributing to the accuracy, speed, and quality of clinical research delivery.

## About the Role

In this role, you will:

- Design and deploy automated workflows to streamline recruitment funnels and administrative processes.
- Transform, clean, and map data across systems and formats (JSON, CSV, Excel) to ensure data integrity.
- Monitor and maintain automation performance, identifying and resolving issues to minimise downtime.
- Troubleshoot and debug workflows and scripts to support operational continuity.
- Maintain clear documentation and version control for automations and integration logic.
- Work across low-code tools and scripting environments to improve efficiency and scalability.
- Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives that reclaim operational capacity.

## About You

You will bring:

- A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Proficiency in Python or a similar scripting language.
- A solid understanding of structured data (e.g. JSON) and data transformation.
- Familiarity with APIs and RESTful services.
- Strong problem-solving skills and an analytical approach to troubleshooting.
- High attention to detail, particularly with data accuracy and system logic.
- A proactive mindset and willingness to learn new technologies.

Ideally, you will also have:

- Exposure to Microsoft Power Automate or Power Apps.
- Experience with version control tools such as Git/GitHub.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g. Azure).
- An interest in AI, automation, and agent-based systems.

## What we offer

- An inclusive environment that supports and values diversity.

- A collaborative, supportive environment where people are trusted to do their jobs well.
- Access to learning, development and career progression as the network continues to grow.
- The chance to do meaningful work that contributes to better treatment options for patients, alongside people who enjoy what they do.
