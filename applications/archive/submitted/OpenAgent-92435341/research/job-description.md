We’re a real estate tech startup that’s reinventing the way property is transacted in Australia. We’re looking for hard-working individuals who will bring their passion and rigour to the OpenAgent team to be a part of its growth and success.  The RoleWe are seeking a Junior Software Engineer to join our team and assist in designing, coding, and testing software applications. In this role, you will collaborate with senior engineers to troubleshoot and resolve issues, participate in the development of new features, and enhance software performance. Your enthusiasm for quality software and fresh perspectives will be instrumental. You will collaborate closely with Engineering, Product, and Marketing teams.Your primary duties will include:
Participate in the technical design and development of our websites, web apps, backend APIs and other backend services
Building new applications as well as improving upon existing ones
Use AI coding assistants (e.g. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) as a core part of your daily workflow to design, write, and test code efficiently
Working closely with the product, marketing, and engineering teams to define, refine, test, and deliver new solutions
Communicate progress and seek guidance on technical issues from senior engineers
Engage in code reviews to ensure code quality, adherence to best practices, and alignment with project standards.
Communicate effectively with stakeholders to gather requirements, provide progress updates, and address any issues.
Collaborate with team members to troubleshoot and resolve bugs and performance issues.
Contribute to documentation and knowledge sharing within the team to support ongoing maintenance and future development.
