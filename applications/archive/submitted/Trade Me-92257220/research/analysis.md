Traditional Engineering (Senior Level)

Professional Senior software engineering experience 
Strong architectural judgement: can shape approaches, trade-offs, integration boundaries, and technical direction on client engagements
Comfortable owning delivery from problem to working software
Experience using AI-assisted development utilising tools like Cursor, Claude Code, or Copilot-class tools. Insight to know when to trust, verify, and rewrite AI outputs.


Hands-on experience embedding LLMs in applications/workflows. Practical understanding of LLM limitations


Naturally articulate in technical conversations with engineers and stakeholders, with the ability to explain trade-offs and progress clearly. 
Comfortable in enterprise contexts


Deriving your absolute technical credibility from active, hands-on building rather than slide-only advisory work. 
Teaching by doing: working alongside peers to leave client teams significantly more capable and self-sufficient over time, not dependent.
