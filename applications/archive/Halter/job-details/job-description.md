# Job Description - Firmware Internship 2025/2026 at Halter

## Company Overview
**Halter** is an agricultural technology company creating virtual fencing systems for cattle. Their mission is to enable farmers to run the most productive and sustainable farms. The company is backed by Tier 1 investors including Bond Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners, DCVC, Blackbird, Promus Ventures, Rocket Lab's Peter Beck and Icehouse ventures.

## Position Details
- **Role**: Firmware Internship 2025/2026
- **Team**: Platform Team (Firmware)
- **Duration**: Summer Internship
- **Location**: Auckland, Auckland, New Zealand
- **Work Model**: Office-first culture (not office-only), high-trust environment, working from office everyday as default with flexibility when needed
- **Industry**: Technology, Information and Internet
- **Employment Type**: Internship

## Role Description
Join the firmware team for summer internship as part of platform team. Work on development of firmware and tooling, taking work from concept to production in live fleet. Collaborate with engineering teams in fast-paced, dynamic environment.

## Key Responsibilities

### What Your Day Could Look Like:
- Design, implement, and deliver new features and performance improvements for product firmware
- Identify, debug, and resolve complex technical issues
- Drive improvements to quality and testing systems
- Proactively monitor performance of live fleet and enhance monitoring platforms

## Technical Context (Agricultural IoT)
Virtual fencing collars for cattle require:
- **Ultra-low power consumption** for extended battery life
- **Robust wireless connectivity** in remote farm environments
- **Real-time GPS tracking** and geofencing capabilities
- **Weather-resistant design** for harsh outdoor conditions
- **Solar charging integration** for sustainable power
- **Animal behavior monitoring** through sensors and algorithms
- **Edge computing** for immediate response to animal movements
- **Fleet management** with thousands of devices in production
