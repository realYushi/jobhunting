# Halter Culture & Values Analysis

## Core Values

### 1. "Farmers First, Animals Always"
- **Origin:** Shaped by their first customer "Frank" who trusted them in 2019
- **Application:** Customer-centric culture starting with farmer experience and working backwards
- **Mission alignment:** Balancing progress with care for both farmers and their livestock
- **Impact:** Happy cows and energized farmers who love what they do

### 2. "Be a Founder"
- **Ownership mentality:** Hire people who think and act like great founders
- **Decision framework:** "Make decisions as if you're the founder"
- **Accountability:** "If you wouldn't want to explain your decision to the entire team, it's probably not the right one"
- **Empowerment:** Take initiative, make decisions, and drive impact without unnecessary rules

### 3. "Max Velocity"
- **Speed to learn:** Move swiftly to learn fast, identify risks early, outpace competition
- **Precision focus:** "Moving quickly in the wrong direction is as good as standing still"
- **Decision filter:** "Does my decision make Halter move faster?"
- **Alignment:** Clear direction and context enabling quick, precise action

### 4. "Bold, Informed Bets"
- **Innovation mindset:** "We don't take no for an answer"
- **Risk philosophy:** Take calculated risks, proactively place bets, work smartly to tip odds
- **Learning approach:** Not every bet pays off, but cost of not taking them is higher
- **Question framework:** Start by asking "How might we?"

### 5. "It's a Privilege"
- **Gratitude culture:** "I get to" rather than "I have to" (inspired by former intern Lona)
- **Purpose-driven:** We get to help farmers, change an industry, work on impactful products
- **Meaning:** Emphasis that "our work matters"

## Work Environment

### Office-First Culture
- **Philosophy:** "No substitute for being co-located, in-person, as much as we can"
- **Benefits:** Real connection with people, high impact, fast innovation, maximum growth
- **Location:** Auckland headquarters with satellite offices
- **Collaboration:** Strong emphasis on in-person teamwork and innovation

### High-Performance Team
- **Standards:** "Not your normal 9 to 5" - high-performing, mission-driven team
- **Commitment:** Team members care deeply about mission and impact, not just collecting paychecks
- **Excellence:** "We pride ourselves on not settling for less than excellence"
- **Meaningful work:** People doing "the best, most meaningful work of their lives"

### Learning & Growth Culture
- **Rapid advancement:** Multiple examples of fast career progression
- **Potential focus:** Emphasis on recognizing potential over qualifications
- **Cross-functional mobility:** Examples of people switching between different functions
- **Mentorship:** Strong internal development and career growth support

## Company History & Stories

### Founding Story
- **Founder:** Craig Piggott, grew up on 300-cow dairy farm in Matamata
- **Background:** Rocket Lab engineer who saw opportunity to bring tech to agriculture
- **Mission:** "If they could build rockets from New Zealand, why was no one bringing tech to ag?"
- **Reality check:** "I thought we could raise $150,000, hire one person, and have the first farm running. That was laughably wrong."

### Cultural Milestones
- **"Big Bird" demo:** Remote-controlled cow demonstration to San Francisco investors
- **"Cali Fest":** Company-wide collar calibration festivals with DJ, snacks, and team spirit
- **Winter survival:** All-hands collar swapping operations during harsh winters
- **First install:** Hand-building towers and manually mixing 2000kg of concrete

### Alumni Success
- **Max Olson:** Co-founder/CTO → Founded Vessev (backed by Blackbird)
- **George Howes:** Designer → Founded MagicBrief
- Career launchpad reputation for entrepreneurship

## Communication Style

### Internal Communication
- **Transparency:** Open about challenges and learnings
- **Humor:** Self-deprecating stories about early struggles
- **Authenticity:** Honest about being "laughably wrong" initially
- **Celebration:** Recognition of team member achievements and growth

### External Messaging
- **Farmer-focused:** Always leads with farmer and animal benefits
- **Impact-driven:** Emphasis on meaningful change in agriculture
- **Technology-enabled:** Position as tech company solving real-world problems
- **Growth story:** Confidence in expansion and scaling

## Leadership Style

### Executive Approach
- **Accessible:** Leaders share personal stories and lessons learned
- **Growth-oriented:** Multiple internal promotions to executive level
- **Mission-driven:** All decisions filtered through farmer impact
- **Innovation-focused:** Willingness to take risks and learn from failures

### Decision Making
- **Data-driven:** Strong metrics focus on farmer outcomes
- **Collaborative:** Team input valued across all levels
- **Fast-paced:** Quick decision-making aligned with "max velocity" value
- **Customer-centric:** Farmer feedback directly influences product development
