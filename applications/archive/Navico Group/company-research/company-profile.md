# Company Profile: Navico Group

## Company Overview
**Name**: Navico Group  
**Parent Company**: Brunswick Corporation (Fortune 500)  
**Industry**: Marine Electronics & Navigation Technology  
**Headquarters**: Global presence with operations worldwide  
**Market Position**: Leading provider of marine electronics for recreational and commercial vessels  

## Core Business & Mission
Navico Group is comprised of the world's leading brands in marine, RV, and specialty vehicle industries. Their mission is "Taking you further" through seamlessly integrated solutions that enable greater possibilities on the water, at the campsite, and beyond.

**Key Value Proposition**: Unifying the most trusted products and brands into integrated solutions for simple, plug-and-play connections and seamless user experiences.

## Key Brands Portfolio
### Marine Electronics (Primary Focus)
- **Lowrance®**: Fishing electronics and navigation systems
- **Simrad®**: Professional marine electronics and autopilot systems  
- **B&G®**: Sailing instrumentation and navigation technology
- **C-MAP®**: Digital marine charts and mapping solutions

### Power & Digital Systems
- **CZONE®**: Digital switching and monitoring systems
- **Mastervolt**: Power conversion and battery management
- **Blue Sea® Systems**: Electrical components and circuit protection
- **RELiON®**: Lithium battery technology

### Performance Components
- **Attwood®**: Marine hardware and accessories
- **Whale®**: Pumps and plumbing systems
- **Lenco**: Trim tabs and marine actuators
- **Marinco®**: Electrical connectors and shore power

## Technology Focus Areas
### Marine Electronics Innovation
- **Advanced Navigation Systems**: GPS, chartplotters, and fish finders
- **Integrated Ecosystems**: Seamless connectivity between marine electronics
- **Real-time Data Processing**: Sonar, radar, and sensor integration
- **Safety-Critical Systems**: Navigation aids for vessel safety

### Digital Integration
- **Hardware-Software Integration**: Embedded systems with user interfaces
- **Marine Protocols**: NMEA 2000, NMEA 0183 data communication
- **Cross-Platform Development**: Solutions across different marine electronics
- **IoT Connectivity**: Connected marine systems and remote monitoring
