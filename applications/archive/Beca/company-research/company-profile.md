# Beca - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded**: 1920 (Over 100 years of operation)  
**Industry**: Engineering, Design & Advisory Consultancy  
**Location**: Headquarters in Auckland, New Zealand  
**Global Reach**: 25 offices worldwide, projects in 70+ countries  
**Employee Count**: 4,000+ employees globally  
**Status**: Privately held, employee-ownership model

## Business Profile

Beca is one of Asia Pacific's largest independent advisory, design, and engineering consultancies. Grown from a family-owned business to one of the most progressive, client-centric professional services consultancies in the region.

### Core Services

-   **Advisory**: Strategic consulting and business advisory services
-   **Design**: Engineering design across multiple disciplines
-   **Engineering**: Technical engineering solutions and project delivery
-   **Project & Cost Management**: End-to-end project delivery
-   **Digital Services**: Technology and digital transformation solutions
