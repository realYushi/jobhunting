Do you want to be the driving force for exceptional UX and UI designs across some of Australasia's leading companies?

At Sandfield, we build smart software solutions for top brands, and we're looking for a talented experienced Web Developer - UX/UI Design Specialist with at least five years of experience to join our team. We pride ourselves on being a leading, Kiwi-owned software business that isn't just a "conventional" tech company.

The Role

In this role, you will be at the heart of our mission to deliver exceptional digital experiences through our internal products and bespoke solutions. You will collaborate with our team of over 100 developers to help them deliver user experiences that give our customers the edge.
