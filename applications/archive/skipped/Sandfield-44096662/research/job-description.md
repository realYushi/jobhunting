About the job

Do you want to be the driving force for exceptional UX and UI designs across some of Australasia's leading companies?

At Sandfield, we build smart software solutions for top brands, and we're looking for a talented experienced Web Developer - UX/UI Design Specialist with at least five years of experience to join our team. We pride ourselves on being a leading, Kiwi-owned software business that isn't just a "conventional" tech company.

The Role

In this role, you will be at the heart of our mission to deliver exceptional digital experiences through our internal products and bespoke solutions. You will collaborate with our team of over 100 developers to help them deliver user experiences that give our customers the edge.

Key Responsibilities


Design & UX Architecture: Design intuitive user interfaces and craft seamless user journeys for diverse digital products
Research & Optimisation: Conduct user research and usability testing to gather insights and refine design solutions using data
AI-accelerated workflows: Explore and introduce AI-accelerated design workflows, from using LLMs to build and document design systems, to building AI-driven interactive prototypes that make requirements gathering more engaging
(AI) Prototyping: Create detailed wireframes, interactive prototypes, and design mockups to guide development teams and stakeholders
Web Performance & SEO: Plan and structure web content and navigation to enhance search engine visibility and user findability
Technical Documentation: Produce high-fidelity documentation and functional specifications, including interaction logic, responsive breakpoints, and design tokens
Quality Assurance: Oversee the transition of designs into live web environments, performing QA to ensure visual and functional integrity
Lifecycle Management: Manage the design process from initial brief through to live web environments, performing quality assurance to ensure visual and functional integrity. 
Mentorship: Provide mentorship to other designers, helping them grow their skills and contribute effectively to the team


Expertise & Requirements


Figma Mastery: Expert-level proficiency in Figma for efficient prototyping and collaborative design
Front-end Literacy: A strong understanding of HTML5, CSS3, and modern web frameworks to ensure design feasibility and smooth developer hand-off
Cross-Platform Design: Proven ability to ensure cohesive experiences across mobile and web platforms
Technical Collaboration: Expertise in collaborating with developers to optimise designs for web performance, accessibility, and cross-browser compatibility
Business Alignment: Ability to collaborate with product managers and stakeholders to ensure designs meet both user needs and business objectives
Human-Computer Interaction: Deep understanding HCI theories and the ability to apply these principles to optimise the interaction between users and software, ensuring interfaces are not only aesthetic but also cognitively efficient and accessible. 
Adaptability: A hands-on approach with the ability to adapt to different budgets and requirements across a variety of projects


What's in it for you?


Fast-track your career: We reward initiative and performance with rapid advancement opportunities
Never a dull moment: Enjoy diverse projects and challenges
Positive vibes only: Our flat team structure fosters a collaborative and energetic atmosphere
Work your way: Benefit from flexible working arrangements
Grow beyond coding: Develop valuable soft skills through dedicated training
Have some fun: Enjoy regular social events, "Fun Food Evenings," and a well-stocked fridge


We offer a rewarding and engaging work experience where you can grow professionally and personally.

Why work for us?

We've been creating great solutions since 1989, facilitating high-performance supply chains for the likes of Mainfreight and Qube. Our average length of service is over five years because we focus on getting the job done while having a good time.

Note: Please do not apply if you are not currently in NZ (unless you are a New Zealand citizen).
