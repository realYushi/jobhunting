Join Vista Group as a Graduate Software Engineer in Auckland, working with cutting-edge cinema software technologies in a hybrid environment.

Your Role

Key responsibilities include:

Developing and maintaining software solutions for cinema websites, mobile apps, loyalty programs, digital signage, POS systems, kiosks, and ticket scanning.
Working on backend and frontend components involving scheduling films and trailers, vouchers, gift cards, refunds, payments, and staff management.
Collaborating with teams using cloud-based platforms and modern deployment tools.
About You

The company is looking for:

Recent graduates available to start immediately.
Familiarity or willingness to work with the following tech stack: C#, .Net 6, SQL Server, Angular/Typescript/React, Docker, Kubernetes, Octopus, Microsoft Azure and/or AWS.
Compensation & Benefits
Excellent work/life balance including a 4 ½ day working week.
Hybrid working arrangement with regular weekly attendance in the Auckland office.
Medical and life insurance.
Volunteer day, enhanced paid parental leave, and wellness benefits.
Fun team events including the Vista Innovation Cup.
Training & Development
Strong mentoring and career development focus.
Career Progression

Opportunities to grow within a dynamic and collaborative team environment aligned with Vista Group’s Shared Standards and vision.
