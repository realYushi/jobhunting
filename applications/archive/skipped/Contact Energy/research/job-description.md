## Te Whiwhinga mahi | The opportunity

At Te Mata Hiko (Contact Energy), our graduates don’t sit on the sidelines — they’re embedded in real teams, working on real challenges that help shape Aotearoa’s renewable energy future.

Our 18-month Graduate Development Programme is designed to give you hands-on experience, meaningful responsibility, and the support you need to grow into a permanent role within the business.

Whether you’re passionate about engineering, technology, data, projects or law, you’ll play a part in helping us decarbonise Aotearoa and build a smarter, more sustainable energy system.

- A permanent role from day one
- An 18-month rotational programme, starting February 2027
- Hands-on work on live assets, projects and systems
- Learning, mentorship and support through Contact University
- A strong graduate cohort and access to experienced leaders
- Flexibility, great benefits, and a genuinely inclusive culture

## Software Engineer Graduate | Wellington / Auckland

You’ll be embedded in Agile squads, contributing to real systems that support Contact’s technology landscape — from core platforms to customer-facing solutions.
