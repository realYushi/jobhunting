About Accenture

At Accenture, we believe in the power of creativity, technology, and innovation to transform the way the world works and lives. As a global leader in professional services, we deliver cutting-edge solutions in marketing, design, technology, and operations. Our people are the driving force behind our success, creating value and delivering transformative change for our clients.

Who Is Accenture Song, you ask?

At Accenture Song, we’re redefining the way brands connect with people. As the world’s largest digital agency, we combine creativity, technology, and commerce to deliver experiences that inspire, engage, and transform. From business reinvention to launching new products and creating compelling campaigns, we solve our clients’ toughest challenges.

When you join Accenture Song, you’ll be part of a team shaping the future of customer experiences and digital innovation. You’ll work on impactful projects, collaborate with creative minds, and make a difference from day one.

Your Opportunity to Shape the Future

As a Graduate / Analyst in Song, you'll work on groundbreaking projects, collaborate with industry leaders, and develop skills that will empower you to make a real impact.

Pros and cons of working at Accenture Australia and New Zealand
Pros

The people here are a great community.

The working arrangements of working from home is very beneﬁcial for my lifestyle. There are many programs being held for employees from a variety of industries to enhance career development.

Cons

Only downside I've seen is potential delays on promotion and difficulty with deadlines.

Pay is average, role and work depend on the project that you land on.
