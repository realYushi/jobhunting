Junior Software Engineer Graduate Program 2026 

Kia ora, hello, hola, 你好, こんにちは, 

Fast-track your tech career with a leading insurance and financial services organisation in 2026. 

Launch your software engineering career working on real systems used by millions across Australia and New Zealand. This is a hands-on graduate role where you’ll learn fast, build real products, and grow your career from day one. 

Why this role is a big deal 
Real impact: Work on large-scale, business-critical insurance platforms 
Fast growth: Structured training plus on-the-job learning with senior engineers 
No experience required: Full technical training provided 
Strong support: Mentors, coaches, and ongoing career guidance 
Clear pathway: Build a long-term career in Software Engineering 
The graduate journey 

First 12 weeks: FDM Skills Lab 

Full-time technical training in Java, Spring, SQL, and core engineering fundamentals
Hands-on projects to build confidence before joining your client
Learn how real enterprise tech teams work 

Client assignment 

Join the technology team of a top ASX-listed insurance client
Receive training on a leading enterprise insurance platform
Work on systems that power policies, claims, and billing
Keep learning through continuous upskilling while on assignment 

What you’ll be doing 

Building, testing, and maintaining enterprise applications
Writing clean, maintainable code and taking part in code reviews
Developing and consuming REST APIs
Collaborating with engineers, analysts, and QA in agile teams
Learning how large-scale systems work in the real world 

About you 

Bachelor’s degree in any discipline
Curious, motivated, and keen to learn
Strong communication and problem-solving skills
Must have full working rights in New Zealand (New Zealand Citizen or Australian Resident Visa)
Willingness to work on client site in Auckland or Wellington
Comfortable working in the office 3–5 days per week, depending on the client 

Start dates: February | March | April 2026
