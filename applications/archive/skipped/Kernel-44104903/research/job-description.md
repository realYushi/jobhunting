About the job

About The Role

Kernel is growing and we’re building capability in how AI and automation is used across the business.

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys turning messy, manual workflows into clear, scalable systems. You’ll work closely with the CTO and teams across the business to understand how work gets done today, identify where time is lost, and implement practical solutions that improve speed, quality, and efficiency.

This isn’t a research role or a “side project” for AI - it’s about real adoption and measurable improvements in how the business operates.

About Kernel

We're a 100% Kiwi owned and operated fintech on a mission to make wealth accessible for every Kiwi. Our investment platform takes the guesswork out of investing – offering KiwiSaver, Managed Funds, US shares & ETFs, and Smart Saver accounts, all in one place with some of the lowest fees in the market.

We're at an exciting stage. Scaling fast, expanding our team of globally experienced financial professionals, and building the foundations for what's next. Our culture is collaborative, hands-on and ambitious – built on trust, innovation and saying it like it is. We value diverse perspectives, empower individuality, and believe the best ideas come from thinking together.

Things move quickly here, and every person plays a key role in shaping our future.

What You’ll Do


Map and document workflows across teams - understanding what’s happening, where it breaks, and where work is repeated
Identify opportunities to automate or augment work using AI, knowing what should be handled by people vs technology
Implement automations using tools like Zapier, Make, n8n (or equivalents) and AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor etc.)
Work alongside engineers where deeper integrations or API work is required
Support teams to adopt new ways of working, influencing a culture of experimentation and continuous improvement


You’ll be great if you bring


Experience building automations that are used in real environments, not just prototypes
Practical experience with AI tools and an understanding of how they can be applied in business workflows
Strong systems thinking - you can break down and improve how work gets done
Comfort working with APIs, integrations, and technical concepts
Confidence working across both technical and non-technical teams


Bonus things you could bring along to the role


Experience working in a startup, scale-up, or fast-moving environment
Exposure to LLM-enabled workflows or building multi-step automation processes
Basic scripting or development capability (e.g. Python, JavaScript)
Experience working with CRMs, internal tools, or operational systems


Why You’ll Love it at Kernel


Play a key role in shaping how AI and automation is used across the business
Have real impact - your work will directly improve how teams operate day-to-day
Work closely with senior leadership and influence how the business evolves
Competitive compensation package
Enjoy the best of both worlds with a flexible hybrid setup, connect in person while keeping the freedom to work from home up to couple of days each week (minimum 3 days/week in the office).
Health insurance, parental leave top-up, and professional development support
Extra staff holidays, office snacks, coffee, and a dog-friendly environment
