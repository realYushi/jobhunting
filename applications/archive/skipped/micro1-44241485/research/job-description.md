About the job

Job Title: Frontend Developer (HTML, CSS)




Job Type: Contractor




Location: Remote




Job Summary: In this role, you'll apply your expertise to help train next-generation AI systems. Your work will shape how models learn, reason, and perform through high-quality, real-world input. No prior experience in AI is required — your domain knowledge is what matters.




Key Responsibilities:

• Translate design files into clean, responsive, and reusable UI components using modern frontend technologies.

• Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers and backend developers to deliver integrated, high-quality solutions.

• Implement and optimize front-end logic using Angular, Vue.js, React, Bootstrap, and JQuery.

• Develop and maintain scalable UI components with a focus on performance and accessibility.

• Integrate front-end applications with APIs and cloud platforms to power dynamic, data-driven experiences.

• Execute unit and automated tests to ensure code reliability and maintainability.

• Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve complex front-end issues effectively.




Required Skills and Qualifications:

• Expert-level proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

• Hands-on experience with frameworks such as Angular, Vue.js, React, and Bootstrap.

• Solid understanding of working with design files and converting them into interactive interfaces.

• Proficient in integrating back-end services and APIs, especially with CakePHP and MySQL.

• Strong background in developing scalable UI components and modular codebases.

• Experience with unit and automation testing methodologies.

• Excellent problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.




Preferred Qualifications:

• Experience working with cloud platforms for web application deployment.

• Familiarity with advanced debugging tools and performance optimization techniques.

• Prior experience in large-scale, customer-focused software projects.
