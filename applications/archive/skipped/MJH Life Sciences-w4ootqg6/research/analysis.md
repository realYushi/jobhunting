Job Description

At MJH Life Sciences our success is measured by your success! If you set your standards high and want to contribute to a winning team, we’ll provide you with every opportunity to help grow our company and your career. Our associates come from all backgrounds, sharing one key quality: determination to succeed. We value being Service Focused, having a Passion for Winning, Innovation, Respect, Integrity, and Teamwork. Nothing means more to us than hiring people with these attributes. If you believe you’re right for the job, this is the place to prove it!

We are seeking an Associate Product Engineer to join our Enterprise Platforms team — the group that builds the internal systems connecting MJH's business-critical platforms into cohesive, automated workflows. You will contribute to features across the full stack, from frontend UI to backend APIs to cloud infrastructure, with mentorship and support from senior engineers along the way. This role is designed for an early-career engineer who is curious, ships working software, and wants to grow fast in a high-impact environment where the work you build is used every day by real teams across the organization. You are expected to use AI-powered developer tools as a core part of how you write code, learn, and deliver.

What You'll Do

Build and ship features across Next.js applications, serverless APIs, and internal CLI tools with guidance from senior engineers
Use AI-powered developer tools — including Claude Code, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot — as a standard part of your daily workflow to move faster, write better code, and accelerate learning
Contribute to integrations with enterprise SaaS platforms including Salesforce, Workfront, Workday, and Bizzabo via REST APIs and webhook pipelines, picking up OAuth flows and API patterns as you go
Help build tools and services that empower internal teams to safely interact with enterprise platform data
Participate in designing and implementing event-driven automation workflows using webhooks, queues, and database-backed state machines
Explore practical applications of AI and LLM capabilities — including content extraction and conversational interfaces — under the guidance of senior teamm
