Sandfield stood out because you build bespoke software around real business problems—supply chains, integrations, finance, and custom development—rather than one-size-fits-all products. That means the job is as much about understanding the "why" as writing code, which is the kind of engineering I want to keep doing. I also like that you work directly with clients and are open to AI tools where they genuinely speed up delivery.

In my current role at GrowLab Technologies, I build internal tools and client-facing applications with React, TypeScript, Node.js, and .NET, and I take ideas from prototype to production-ready features. I would be comfortable learning deeper into your stack, working directly with customers, and using AI tools as part of a careful development process rather than a shortcut.
