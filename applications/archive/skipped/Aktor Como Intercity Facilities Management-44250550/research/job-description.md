About the job

🗂 We’re Hiring: Entry-Level Software Developer

🕒 Employment Type: Full-Time

💼 Level: Entry-Level

We’re looking for a motivated and enthusiastic Entry-Level Software Developer to join our growing team. This role is ideal for recent graduates, junior developers, or aspiring software engineers who are eager to build their technical skills, contribute to real-world projects, and grow their careers in software development.

🎯 Key Responsibilities

• Assist in the design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of software applications.

• Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code under the guidance of senior developers and technical leads.

• Participate in code reviews and collaborate with team members to improve software quality and development practices.

• Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve software issues and technical defects.

• Support the development and integration of new features, enhancements, and system functionalities.

• Collaborate with developers, designers, business analysts, and project stakeholders to deliver project requirements.

• Conduct software testing and ensure applications meet quality, performance, and security standards.

• Maintain technical documentation, development records, and project updates.

• Learn and apply software engineering best practices, development tools, and emerging technologies.

• Contribute innovative ideas and solutions to improve products, processes, and user experiences.

✅ Requirements

• Basic knowledge of programming languages such as Java, Python, JavaScript, C#, PHP, or similar technologies.

• Understanding of software development concepts, object-oriented programming (OOP), and coding best practices.

• Familiarity with databases, APIs, web technologies, and version control systems such as Git is an advantage.

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting abilities.

• Willingness to learn new technologies and adapt to a fast-paced development environment.

• Good communication and teamwork skills.

• Strong attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality work.

• Internship experience, academic projects, hackathons, or personal software development projects are advantageous but not required.

• Bachelor’s degree or diploma in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field is preferred.

🌟 What We Offer

• A supportive and collaborative learning environment.

• Hands-on experience working on real-world software development projects.

• Mentorship and guidance from experienced developers and technical leaders.

• Opportunities for professional development and career advancement.

• Exposure to modern technologies, frameworks, cloud platforms, and software engineering best practices.

• Competitive compensation and benefits package.
