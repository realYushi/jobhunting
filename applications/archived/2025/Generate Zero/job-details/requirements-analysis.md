# Requirements Analysis - Software Developer (Generate Zero)

## Critical Requirements (Must Have)

### Technical Foundation
- **Modern Web Development**: Strong proficiency in contemporary web frameworks and languages
  - *Evidence*: Climate tech companies require scalable, responsive applications for sustainability platforms
  - *Priority*: Essential for building customer-facing data visualization tools

- **Full-Stack Capabilities**: Ability to work across frontend and backend systems
  - *Evidence*: Small startup environment requires versatile developers who can contribute across the stack
  - *Priority*: Critical for early-stage team efficiency

- **Data Handling & Visualization**: Experience with data processing and creating intuitive dashboards
  - *Evidence*: Core business involves carbon tracking, reporting, and analytics for enterprise clients
  - *Priority*: Essential for sustainability measurement platforms

### Professional Qualities
- **Mission Alignment**: Genuine passion for environmental impact and sustainability
  - *Evidence*: Employee testimonial: "The company's mission to combat climate change aligns perfectly with my values"
  - *Priority*: Critical for cultural fit and long-term retention

- **Collaboration Skills**: Ability to work in flat organizational structure
  - *Evidence*: Company culture emphasizes "collective success" and "everyone operates on same level"
  - *Priority*: Essential for team dynamics and communication

- **Learning Agility**: Continuous improvement mindset and adaptability
  - *Evidence*: "Continuous innovation" core value and personalized growth plans mentioned
  - *Priority*: Critical for startup environment and evolving climate tech landscape

## Preferred Requirements (Nice to Have)

### Technical Enhancements
- **Cloud Platform Experience**: Familiarity with Azure, AWS, or similar platforms
  - *Evidence*: Enterprise clients likely require scalable, secure cloud solutions
  - *Priority*: Valuable for deployment and infrastructure management

- **API Development**: Experience building and integrating APIs
  - *Evidence*: Sustainability platforms require integration with various data sources
  - *Priority*: Important for enterprise client integrations

- **DevOps Practices**: CI/CD, containerization, automation experience
  - *Evidence*: Growing startup needs efficient deployment and scaling practices
  - *Priority*: Beneficial for development efficiency

### Domain Knowledge
- **Enterprise Software Experience**: Understanding of business-to-business software requirements
  - *Evidence*: Client base includes banks, insurance, government, and large enterprises
  - *Priority*: Valuable for understanding client needs and requirements

- **Data Analytics Background**: Understanding of measurement, reporting, and compliance frameworks
  - *Evidence*: Core business involves carbon footprint measurement and sustainability reporting
  - *Priority*: Beneficial for understanding product context

## Implicit Requirements

### Climate Tech Industry Knowledge
- **Sustainability Awareness**: Understanding of environmental regulations, carbon accounting, and ESG frameworks
  - *Context*: Helps in building relevant solutions and communicating with environmentally-focused clients
  - *Impact*: Enables better product decisions and client interactions

### Startup Environment Adaptability
- **Wearing Multiple Hats**: Willingness to take on various responsibilities beyond core development
  - *Context*: Small team environment where everyone contributes across different areas
  - *Impact*: Essential for startup agility and growth

- **Client-Facing Comfort**: Ability to interact with enterprise clients and understand business requirements
  - *Context*: Small team may require developers to participate in client discussions
  - *Impact*: Important for gathering requirements and providing technical guidance

### Cultural Integration
- **Social Engagement**: Participation in company culture and team activities
  - *Context*: Company emphasizes active social calendar and team bonding
  - *Impact*: Important for team cohesion and cultural fit

## Experience Level Expectations

### Seniority Level
- **Junior to Mid-Level**: 1-4 years of experience preferred
  - *Evidence*: Current team includes "Intermediate Software Developer" and "Software Developer" roles
  - *Rationale*: Growing company needs developers who can grow with the organization

### Specific Contexts
- **Real-World Application**: Experience with production systems and live user bases
- **Team Collaboration**: Experience working in collaborative development environments
- **Modern Development Practices**: Familiarity with current development workflows and tools

## Technology Stack Focus

### Primary Technologies (Inferred)
- **Frontend**: Modern JavaScript frameworks (React, Vue.js, Angular)
- **Backend**: Python, Node.js, or .NET for API development
- **Databases**: SQL and NoSQL solutions for large datasets
- **Cloud**: Azure or AWS for enterprise-grade hosting
- **DevOps**: CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker)

### Integration Requirements
- **API Frameworks**: RESTful services, potentially GraphQL
- **Data Processing**: ETL pipelines for sustainability data
- **Visualization**: Charts, dashboards, and reporting tools
- **Security**: Enterprise-grade security for sensitive business data

## Growth and Development Focus

### Technical Growth
- **Climate Tech Specialization**: Opportunity to become expert in sustainability technology
- **Enterprise Solutions**: Learning to build scalable B2B platforms
- **Data Science Integration**: Potential exposure to analytics and machine learning

### Career Progression
- **Leadership Opportunities**: Growth into technical leadership roles
- **Product Ownership**: Involvement in product strategy and client relationships
- **Sustainability Expertise**: Becoming a thought leader in climate tech solutions
