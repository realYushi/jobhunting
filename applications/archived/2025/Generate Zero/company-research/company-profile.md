# Generate Zero - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 2023  
**Location:** Auckland, New Zealand  
**Industry:** Climate Technology / Sustainability Software  
**Company Stage:** Growth-stage startup  
**Size:** 15-20 employees  

## Mission & Vision

Generate Zero is passionate about creating a sustainable future, believing that we owe it to future generations to do all we can, while we can. Their digital tools help organizations move from simply measuring GHG emissions to actively managing them.

**Mission:** To empower organizations to accelerate the fight against climate change through data and technology.

**Vision:** Partnering with like-minded organizations to support their decarbonisation journeys and drive the global transition to net zero.

## Business Focus

### Core Platform
"The Revolutionary Sustainability Platform" - An AI-powered platform for comprehensive carbon emissions measurement, reporting, and reduction planning.

### Key Solutions
1. **AI-powered Data Ingestion** - Automated scope 1, 2, and 3 data collection from multiple sources
2. **Emissions Visualization** - Intuitive dashboards for understanding emission sources and tracking progress
3. **Science-Based Target Setting** - Create aligned targets at organization or activity level
4. **Automated Reporting** - Generate inventory and custom reports for audit and compliance
5. **In-platform Audit** - Streamlined verification with dedicated auditor workflow
6. **Financed & Insured Emissions** - Specialized measurement across asset classes and insurance segments

### Industry Specialization
Generate Zero offers tailored solutions for four key sectors:
- **Enterprise** - Climate Reporting Entities, operational and supply chain emissions
- **Banking** - Operational and financed emissions measurement and reduction
- **Insurance** - Insurance-associated and operational emissions management
- **Government** - Carbon Neutral Government Program (CNGP) support
