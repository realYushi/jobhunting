Are you interested in working with a cutting-edge robotics R&D group over the summer? Crown Robotic Technology Centre (RTC) is looking for enthusiastic and capable undergraduate students to work on summer internship projects between November 2025 and February 2026.

Do you study mechatronics, electrical and computer engineering, software engineering, computer science, or a related field? If you have experience with or an interest in:

Software: C, C++, Python, Angular, Typescript
Platforms & Tools: Linux, microcontrollers, ROS, Gazebo
Hardware: Prototype (3D printing, electronics), Sensors (RADAR, LiDAR, 3D tracking systems)
Research Topics: Machine learning, LLMs, Knowledge Graphs, RAG, 3D Localisation
How do you apply? Interested candidates are invited to email Sian Phillips (Sian.Phillips@crown.com) with:

-   a cover letter
-   an up-to-date CV
-   an academic transcript (unofficial version)
