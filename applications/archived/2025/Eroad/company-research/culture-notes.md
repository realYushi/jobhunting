# Culture Notes: EROAD

## Work Environment

EROAD is characterised by a **fast-paced and "full-on" environment**. Despite this intensity, employees find there's a **"right balance"**, describing it as "fast paced but stable" and having "a lot of fun events but still get stuff done". The culture strongly promotes having **each team member's voice heard and embracing innovation**.

The company fosters a **collaborative atmosphere**, where colleagues enjoy bouncing ideas off each other to solve complex problems. There's a strong sense of **belonging** among the team, which is remarkably diverse, comprising individuals from **35 different countries**. Employees note that people genuinely care about others and are passionate about what they do. The workplace also offers lighter moments, including "great banter" both virtually and in person, a significant contingent of **table tennis enthusiasts**, and even a budget for **board games for team building sessions**. Coffee enthusiasm is also noted.

## Company Values in Practice

EROAD's stated values are "We learn & grow", "We get it done", "We do what’s right", and "We play as a team". These values are actively reflected in the company's daily operations:

-   **We learn & grow**: EROAD is highly **supportive around learning and growth**, offering employees the chance to learn something new every day. Individuals are encouraged to **expand their skills**, as seen with Dawin moving into Cloud Development, DevOps, and Product Owner roles. Opportunities are provided at all levels, demonstrating significant **trust in its people**.
-   **We get it done**: The fast-paced and challenging nature of projects suggests a focus on execution. Employees "get stuff done" while maintaining a good balance with fun.
-   **We do what’s right**: EROAD's core purpose is "to **deliver intelligence you can trust, for a better world tomorrow**", striving to make a positive impact on society through technology and innovation by improving safety, efficiency, and environmental sustainability. Employees, such as Nishita, articulate that their work "makes a difference to the world and could save lives". The emphasis on providing "reliable, accurate and real-time insight" underscores a commitment to integrity.
-   **We play as a team**: The collaborative spirit, supportive co-workers, diverse team from many countries, and engaging in team activities like board games and table tennis exemplify the team-oriented culture.

## Employee Reviews & Insights

Employees consistently describe EROAD as a **"land of opportunities with many exciting and challenging projects going on"**. A strong theme is the **trust EROAD places in its people** and the significant opportunities offered at all levels. This trust allows individuals to take on responsibilities early in their careers, contributing to rapid learning and growth. Employees love the feeling that their **work makes a real difference** to the world and has the potential to save lives. The team is described as supportive, caring, and passionate, fostering a strong sense of belonging. The balance between a fast-paced work environment and enjoyable social interactions is highly valued.

## Growth Opportunities

Growth opportunities are a prominent feature of EROAD's culture. The company is referred to as a "land of opportunities" where individuals can **learn something new every day**. EROAD is highly **supportive around learning and growth**.

Examples of career progression within the company include:

-   Interns transitioning to **full-time graduate software engineers**, and then advancing to roles such as scrum master, product owner, and eventually **Chapter Lead**.
-   Graduates starting as analytics engineers and progressing to **software engineer** and then **decision scientist** roles.
-   Individuals finding "next steps" within the company, such as a Communications Partner moving into a new role in **Marketing**.
-   Employees like Dawin expanding their skills into areas like **Cloud Development, DevOps, and Product Ownership**.

The "trust EROAD has in its people" and the opportunities offered at "all levels" are key drivers for this rapid learning and career advancement.
