# Serko Software Engineer Internship - Application Strategy

## Executive Summary

Serko represents an exceptional opportunity for a Software Engineer Internship, combining cutting-edge travel technology innovation with a strong commitment to intern development and global growth. The company's recent GetThere acquisition, AI-focused product development, and world-class leadership team create an ideal environment for meaningful technical contribution and professional growth.

## Strategic Positioning Framework

### 1. Company-Candidate Alignment

**Perfect Timing Factors:**
- **Rapid Growth Phase:** GetThere acquisition creating new technical challenges and opportunities
- **Technology Innovation:** Heavy investment in AI, machine learning, and modern architecture
- **Global Expansion:** International perspective and cross-cultural collaboration opportunities
- **Leadership Investment:** Recent hiring of world-class executives from Booking.com, Trade Me, and other tech leaders

**Cultural Fit Indicators:**
- **"Be a Good Human"** - Aligns with diversity, inclusion, and authentic self-expression values
- **"Win Together"** - Strong collaborative mindset and team-oriented approach
- **"Dare to Simplify"** - Focus on elegant solutions and user-centric design
- **"Boldly Go Beyond"** - Innovation mindset and willingness to challenge status quo

### 2. Technical Value Proposition

**High-Impact Contribution Areas:**
- **AI/ML Integration:** Contribute to Intelligent Itinerary and automation initiatives
- **Connected Trip Technology:** Work on patented unified travel experience solutions
- **Mobile-First Development:** Enhance user experiences for business travelers
- **Data Analytics:** Support advanced analytics and business intelligence initiatives
- **Cloud Architecture:** Learn and contribute to modern, scalable platform development

**Technical Learning Opportunities:**
- **Modern Tech Stack:** Cloud-native, microservices, API-first architecture
- **Global Scale:** 4+ million bookings annually across 150+ countries
- **Real-World Impact:** Direct contribution to solutions used by Fortune 500 companies
- **Innovation Culture:** Hackathons, patents, and cutting-edge technology adoption

### 3. Professional Development Advantages

**Mentorship Access:**
- **Simon Young (CTO):** Former Trade Me and Halter executive, technical leadership
- **Matt Weaver (Head of Data):** Former Booking.com data science leader, AI/ML expertise
- **Tarun Phaugat (VP Engineering):** Recent appointment, focus on engineering excellence
- **Matthew Gerrie (COO):** Former Booking.com executive, operational excellence

**Career Growth Indicators:**
- **Internal Promotion Examples:** Simon Young (VP Engineering → CTO)
- **Global Opportunities:** Teams across New Zealand, Australia, China, India, United States
- **Diverse Career Paths:** Product, engineering, data science, business development options
- **Industry Recognition:** Work with award-winning team and innovative solutions
