# Serko Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** Serko Limited  
**Industry:** Travel Technology / SaaS  
**Founded:** 2007 (with roots dating back to Interactive Technologies Limited in 1994)  
**Headquarters:** Auckland, New Zealand  
**Stock Exchange:** NZX and ASX (Ticker: SKO)  
**Global Presence:** New Zealand, Australia, China (Xi'an, Foshan), India (Bengaluru), United States (Minnesota, Dallas)

## Business Model & Market Position

### Core Business
Serko is a global travel technology leader that simplifies corporate travel management through innovative software solutions. The company powers more than 4 million online bookings annually across 150+ countries.

### Key Products & Solutions

1. **GetThere** (Enterprise)
   - #2 online booking tool in North America by market share
   - Serves 9 out of BTN 100 largest travel programs
   - Recently acquired from Sabre (2024)
   - Targets large enterprise corporations globally

2. **Zeno** (Mid-Market)
   - Corporate travel and expense management platform
   - Sold through international network of TMC partners
   - Integrated travel booking and expense management
   - Powers Booking.com for Business

3. **Booking.com for Business** (SME)
   - Powered by Zeno technology
   - 800,000+ companies signed up
   - Free platform with corporate rates and 24/7 support
   - Partnership with Booking Holdings

### Market Reach & Scale
- **4+ million** online bookings per year
- **150+ countries** served globally
- **800,000+** companies using Booking.com for Business
- **600,000+** companies signed up to Booking.com for Business powered by Zeno
