# Serko Culture & Values Analysis

## Core Purpose & Vision

**Purpose:** "We bring people together"  
**Vision:** "To create a connected, frictionless travel experience by building the world's leading business travel marketplace"  
**Mission:** Simplifying the complex world of corporate travel management

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Be a Good Human 🤝
- **Philosophy:** Show up as your true selves
- **Values:** Embrace diversity of people, thought, and culture
- **Action:** Work intentionally to create positive impact
- **Evidence:** Gender pay equity <1%, 20+ ethnicities represented, strong inclusion programs

### 2. Win Together 🏆
- **Philosophy:** Celebrate success as a collaborative journey
- **Values:** Work together as one team
- **Action:** Transform individual ideas into innovative solutions
- **Evidence:** Cross-functional teams, global collaboration across 5+ countries

### 3. Dare to Simplify ⚡
- **Philosophy:** Challenge ourselves to create simplicity where complexity exists
- **Values:** Focus on user experience and elegant solutions
- **Action:** Continuous product refinement and user-centric design
- **Evidence:** Award-winning UX, simplified booking processes, intuitive interfaces

### 4. Boldly Go Beyond 🚀
- **Philosophy:** Challenge the status quo
- **Values:** Make the impossible possible
- **Action:** Innovation for customers, partners, and internal teams
- **Evidence:** Industry-first patents, AI integration, connected trip experiences
