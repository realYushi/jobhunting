# TRV Trading Group - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** TRV Trading Group  
**Website:** www.trvtradinggroup.com  
**Industry:** Proprietary Trading, High-Frequency Trading, Quantitative Finance  
**Location:** 6 Ward Street, New Lynn, Auckland, New Zealand  
**Founded:** Early 1990s (originated in Singapore)  
**Size:** Small team of highly skilled traders, quants, and technologists  

## Business Model & Operations

### Core Business
- **Proprietary Trading:** Pure prop trading firm with no external clients
- **Self-Funded:** All trading conducted with firm's own capital
- **Multi-Strategy Approach:** Focus on statistical arbitrage, market making, and algorithmic trading
- **High-Frequency Trading:** Custom-built systems handling real-time market data and execution

### Key Operational Areas
1. **Statistical Trading Strategies:** Advanced mathematical models on terabytes of market data
2. **Market Making:** Providing liquidity across multiple markets
3. **Risk Management:** Conservative approach following "You have to be able to come to work tomorrow" philosophy
4. **Technology Infrastructure:** Custom-built trading systems from ground up
5. **Market Microstructure Research:** Deep focus on exchange mechanics and execution optimization
