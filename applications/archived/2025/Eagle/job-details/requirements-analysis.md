# Job Requirements Analysis for Eagle Technology - GIS Summer Intern Programme

## Role Overview

-   **Position Level**: Entry-level/Intern
-   **Department**: GIS/Geospatial Technology
-   **Reporting Structure**: Reports to GIS team leads, part of dynamic team environment
-   **Location**: On-site at Auckland, Wellington, or Christchurch offices (no remote work)
-   **Duration**: 10 weeks (mid-November to mid-February with Christmas/New Year break)

## Technical Requirements

### Must-Have Technical Skills

| Skill               | Experience Level            | Context/Usage                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GIS Interest        | Keen interest               | Foundation for learning ArcGIS products and spatial technologies |
| Data Analysis       | Exposure level              | Working with spatial data, analysis and visualization tasks      |
| Problem-Solving     | Strong analytical skills    | Technical challenges and real-world GIS business scenarios       |
| Academic Foundation | Current tertiary enrollment | Ideally GIS, geography, computer science, or related fields      |

### Preferred Technical Skills

| Skill                | Experience Level        | Context/Usage                                            |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| ArcGIS Products      | Any hands-on experience | Core platform used throughout the programme              |
| Spatial Technologies | Basic familiarity       | Understanding of geospatial concepts and applications    |
| Data Visualization   | Exposure/experience     | Creating maps and visual representations of spatial data |
| Programming          | Basic knowledge         | Potential for GIS automation and analysis tasks          |

### Tools & Platforms

-   **Primary Platform**: ArcGIS System (core focus of internship)
-   **Data Management**: Spatial databases, GeoJSON, mapping data
-   **Visualization**: Mapping tools, data visualization platforms
-   **Collaboration**: Team-based project management tools

## Soft Skills & Behavioral Requirements

### Communication & Collaboration

-   **Excellent communication abilities**: Required for client demonstrations and team presentations
-   **Teamwork skills**: Working collaboratively in supportive team environment with motivated professionals
-   **Presentation skills**: Enhanced through programme for client-facing work

### Leadership & Initiative

-   **Self-directed learning**: Ability to work on self-led projects based on passion
-   **Proactive approach**: Taking initiative in learning opportunities and skill development
-   **Professional development mindset**: Commitment to both technical and personal growth

### Problem-Solving & Innovation

-   **Strong analytical skills**: Essential for GIS analysis and spatial problem-solving
-   **Technical problem-solving**: Building skills to handle real-world business scenarios
-   **Critical thinking**: Understanding spatial relationships and data interpretation

## Experience Requirements

-   **Total Experience**: No specific experience required (entry-level internship)
-   **Relevant Experience**: Any exposure to data analysis, mapping, or spatial technologies beneficial
-   **Education**: Currently enrolled in tertiary education (preferably GIS, geography, computer science, related fields)
-   **Work Rights**: NZ Citizen, Permanent Resident, or valid NZ Work Visa for full-time work
-   **Portfolio/Projects**: Academic transcript required, any GIS/mapping projects advantageous

## Company Culture Indicators

-   **Values Alignment**: Family values, community engagement, excellence in service
-   **Work Style**: Collaborative, hands-on learning, supportive team environment
-   **Growth Mindset**: "Endless opportunities to learn", continuous skill development, professional development focus
-   **Community Impact**: Conservation, education, emergency management support

## Key Responsibilities Breakdown

1. **Learning & Development** (40-50% of time)
    - Intensive ArcGIS training and skill building
    - Professional and personal development opportunities
    - Face-to-face and online learning modules
2. **Real-World Projects** (30-40% of time)
    - Working on actual client projects and business scenarios
    - Collaborative projects with team members
    - Self-led projects based on individual interests
3. **Skill Enhancement** (10-20% of time)
    - Technical and problem-solving skill development
    - Communication and presentation skill building
    - Industry insight and career path exploration

## Programme-Specific Requirements

### Application Process

-   **Platform**: Applications only through Prosple website
-   **Documents**: CV, cover letter, academic transcript (unofficial acceptable)
-   **Timeline**: Applications close July 18, 2025
-   **Selection**: Video interview → virtual interview → reference check

### Cover Letter Requirements

-   Why interested in working with GIS technology
-   Why want to join Eagle Technology specifically
-   Relevant skills or experiences for the role
-   Must be written without AI assistance

### CV Requirements

-   Personal statement (who you are, career goals, why applying)
-   Education background
-   Technical skills inventory
-   Work experience (paid, volunteer, or academic)
-   Extra-curricular activities

## Programme Benefits

-   **Compensation**: Paid internship (NZD 51,000 per year, pro-rated)
-   **Learning**: Outstanding professional and personal development opportunities
-   **Experience**: Real-world GIS industry experience
-   **Networking**: Exposure to spatial industry in New Zealand
-   **Career Development**: Insight into potential career paths
-   **Skills**: Both technical and soft skill enhancement

## Red Flags & Concerns

-   **Workload intensity**: Programme involves high-level learning with potential busy periods
-   **No remote work**: Must be comfortable with full-time office presence
-   **Short timelines**: Company culture includes working under pressure with tight deadlines
-   **Technical depth**: Intensive ArcGIS training may be challenging for those without prior GIS exposure
-   **Limited flexibility**: 10-week commitment with specific start/end dates
