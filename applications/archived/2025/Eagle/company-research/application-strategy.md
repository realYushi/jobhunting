# Application Strategy: Eagle Technology - GIS Summer Intern Programme

## Key Points to Emphasize

### Company Alignment

-   **Family Values & Legacy**: Emphasize appreciation for Eagle's 55+ year family-owned heritage and commitment to excellence in IT/GIS services
-   **Community Impact**: Highlight alignment with their community engagement in conservation, education, and emergency management
-   **Learning Culture**: Demonstrate enthusiasm for their "endless opportunities to learn" and continuous skill development approach
-   **New Zealand Focus**: Show understanding of their role as official Esri distributor for NZ and South Pacific

### Programme-Specific Strengths

-   **10-week commitment**: Show readiness for intensive, hands-on learning experience
-   **Real-world application**: Express interest in working on actual client projects and business scenarios
-   **Team collaboration**: Emphasize teamwork skills and ability to work in supportive environment
-   **Professional development**: Highlight desire for both technical and soft skill enhancement

## Skills to Highlight

### Technical Skills (Must-Have)

-   **GIS/ArcGIS experience**: Any hands-on experience with ArcGIS products or similar GIS software
-   **Data analysis & visualization**: Experience with spatial data, mapping, or data visualization tools
-   **Problem-solving**: Analytical thinking and systematic approach to technical challenges
-   **Academic foundation**: Relevant coursework in GIS, geography, computer science, or related fields

### Soft Skills (Equally Important)

-   **Communication**: Written and verbal communication skills for client demonstrations and presentations
-   **Adaptability**: Ability to handle varied daily tasks and short timelines
-   **Learning agility**: Enthusiasm for continuous learning and skill development
-   **Teamwork**: Collaboration skills for working with diverse, motivated professionals

## Company-Specific Keywords

### Technical Terms

-   ArcGIS, GIS, Geographic Information Systems, spatial data, geospatial technology
-   Emergency management, conservation GIS, location intelligence
-   Data visualization, mapping, spatial analysis
-   ETL processes, database management, cloud services

### Cultural Terms

-   Family values, community engagement, excellence in service
-   Professional development, hands-on learning, real-world experience
-   Conservation, education, not-for-profit support
-   New Zealand spatial industry, Esri partnership

## Potential Challenges/Gaps

### Common Concerns

-   **Workload intensity**: Programme involves high-level learning and potential for busy periods
-   **On-site requirement**: Must be comfortable with full-time office presence (no remote work)
-   **Short timelines**: Need to demonstrate ability to work efficiently under pressure
-   **Technical depth**: May need to show readiness for intensive ArcGIS training

### Mitigation Strategies

-   Emphasize time management and organizational skills
-   Show enthusiasm for collaborative, in-person learning environment
-   Demonstrate previous experience managing competing priorities
-   Highlight foundational technical skills and eagerness to build expertise

## Follow-up Strategy

### Application Timeline

-   **Applications close**: July 18, 2025
-   **Review period**: All applications reviewed after closing
-   **Video interview**: If shortlisted, complete short video interview
-   **Virtual interview**: Discussion of background, interests, and alignment
-   **Decision notification**: By phone if successful, email if not selected

### Key Application Components

1. **Cover Letter Focus**:

    - Why interested in GIS technology
    - Why Eagle Technology specifically
    - Relevant skills and experiences

2. **CV Requirements**:
    - Personal statement with career goals
    - Education background
    - Technical skills inventory
    - Work experience (paid/volunteer/academic)
    - Extra-curricular activities

### Post-Application

-   **Prepare for video interview**: Practice articulating GIS interest and Eagle Technology alignment
-   **Research current projects**: Stay updated on Eagle's recent work in emergency management and conservation
-   **Network preparation**: Research Eagle Technology employees on LinkedIn for potential connections
-   **Reference preparation**: Ensure academic and professional references are ready for final stage
