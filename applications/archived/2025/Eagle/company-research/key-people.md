## Company Leadership

Eagle Technology's leadership is deeply rooted in its founding family:

-   **Trevor Eagle**: Co-founder of Eagle Technology in 1969 with his wife Corallie. He was a leader in the information technology industry and known for his support to science, education, community, and environmental organisations. Trevor was posthumously awarded the **Flying Kiwi award** for his outstanding contribution to New Zealand's Hi-Tech industries. His foresight is credited with Eagle Technology becoming a world-leading systems integration and information management company.
-   **Corallie Eagle**: Co-founder of Eagle Technology. She became Chairman after Trevor's death in 2000 and held that position until her passing in April 2021.
-   **Duane Eagle**: The current **Managing Director** of Eagle Technology. He continues the legacy of his parents, Trevor and Corallie, and is committed to the company's founding vision. Duane actively seeks new opportunities as technologies like Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and cloud services integrate, providing "whole care solutions" to customers.

The sources also note that **all seven of Trevor and Corallie’s children have worked within the company at some time**, indicating a strong family involvement in the company's history and operations.

## Notable Team Members

It is also important to note key figures in the broader GIS field, particularly those associated with Esri, as Eagle Technology is the **official Esri distributor for New Zealand and the South Pacific**. These figures include:

-   **Jack Dangermond**: Co-founder of Esri (Environmental Systems Research Institute) in 1969, a pioneer in GIS technology, and credited with inspiring GIS Day.
-   **Laura Dangermond**: Co-founder of Esri.
-   **Roger Tomlinson**: A geographer who coined the term "geographic information system (GIS)" and is known as the "father of GIS".
