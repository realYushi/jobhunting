# Gallagher Group - Culture and Values Analysis

## Core Cultural Identity

**Brand Identity:** #TeamGallagher - emphasizing collective achievement and collaboration  
**Cultural Foundation:** People-first culture extending across global operations  
**Heritage:** 85+ years of family-oriented business values with modern innovation focus

### CEO Vision (Kahl Betham)
*"People working together as a team will enable our business to achieve true global scale, and this focus on company values and culture at Gallagher will sustain our customers, communities, and our families into the future."*

**Key Leadership Principles:**
- High-performance solutions through collaborative teamwork
- Focus on doing the right things for people and communities
- Global scale achievement through cultural strength
- Long-term sustainability over short-term gains
