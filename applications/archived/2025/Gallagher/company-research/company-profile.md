# Gallagher Group Limited - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 1938 (85+ years of innovation)  
**Headquarters:** Hamilton, New Zealand  
**Global Reach:** 160+ countries with Gallagher technology  
**Annual Revenue:** $NZD (specific figure not disclosed)  
**Employees:** 1,300+ team members globally  
**Stock Exchange:** Not publicly listed (private company)

## Business Structure

Gallagher operates as a global technology company with two distinct business divisions:

### 1. Gallagher Security
- **Focus:** Access control, perimeter security, intruder alarms, and cybersecurity
- **Market Position:** Trusted by 20,000+ customers across 140+ countries
- **Industries Served:** 40+ key industries including government, education, healthcare, data centers, utilities
- **Leadership:** Mark Junge, Chief Executive - Security
- **Experience:** 35+ years in security solutions

### 2. Gallagher Animal Management  
- **Focus:** Electric fencing, weighing and EID, data collection, wireless water monitoring
- **Market Position:** World-leading animal management solutions since 1938
- **Innovation Areas:** Virtual fencing (eShepherd), automated weighing, IoT monitoring
- **Leadership:** Lisbeth Jacobs, Chief Executive - Animal Management
- **Specialization:** Responsible, productive, and profitable farming solutions
