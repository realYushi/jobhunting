# Application Strategy: EROAD

## Key Points to Emphasize

### Technical Excellence & Innovation

-   EROAD is at the forefront of transportation technology, being the **first company in the world to implement a GNSS/cellular-based road charging solution across an entire country**
-   Emphasize experience with cutting-edge technologies, IoT devices, telematics, and data analytics
-   Highlight any experience with fleet management, GPS tracking, or transportation technology

### Growth & Learning Mindset

-   Company strongly values "We learn & grow" - position yourself as someone who thrives in learning environments
-   Highlight examples of rapid skill acquisition and adaptability
-   Mention willingness to take on diverse responsibilities (like Dawin expanding into Cloud Development, DevOps, and Product Ownership)

### Global Impact & Purpose-Driven Work

-   Connect with their mission to "deliver intelligence you can trust, for a better world tomorrow"
-   Emphasize desire to work on technology that **"makes a difference to the world and could save lives"**
-   Show alignment with improving safety, efficiency, and environmental sustainability

### Collaborative Team Player

-   Demonstrate strong teamwork and collaboration skills (aligns with "We play as a team")
-   Show ability to work in diverse, multicultural environments (35 countries represented)
-   Highlight experience with cross-functional collaboration and mentoring

## Potential Challenges/Gaps

### Technical Specialization

-   If lacking specific transportation/fleet management experience, focus on transferable skills
-   Emphasize any IoT, mobile, or real-time data processing experience
-   Highlight adaptability and quick learning (company values growth mindset)

### Industry Knowledge

-   Research transportation regulations, especially New Zealand RUC system
-   Understand fleet management challenges and sustainability concerns
-   Learn about telematics industry trends and compliance requirements

### Company Culture Fit

-   Must demonstrate comfort with fast-paced, high-responsibility environment
-   Show evidence of thriving in collaborative, diverse teams
-   Prepare examples of taking initiative and "getting things done"

## Follow-up Strategy

### Application Phase

-   **Research specific role requirements** and map skills accordingly
-   **Connect with current employees** on LinkedIn, especially those in similar roles (Daniel, Dawin, Jess, Nishita)
-   **Customize resume** to highlight relevant technical experience and growth mindset
-   **Tailor cover letter** to emphasize alignment with mission and values

### Interview Preparation

-   Prepare examples demonstrating each of their four values in action
-   Research latest EROAD product developments and industry challenges
-   Prepare thoughtful questions about growth opportunities and technical challenges
-   Show genuine interest in transportation technology and global impact

### Post-Application

-   **Follow up appropriately** with hiring manager or team leads
-   **Engage with EROAD content** on social media to show continued interest
-   **Stay updated** on company news and product launches
-   **Network strategically** with employees in target department

### Long-term Relationship Building

-   Even if not successful initially, maintain professional relationships
-   Continue following company developments and industry trends
-   Position for future opportunities as the company grows globally
