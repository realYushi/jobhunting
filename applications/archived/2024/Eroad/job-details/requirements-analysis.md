# Job Requirements Analysis for EROAD - Summer Internship Programme (Software/Firmware Engineers)

## Role Overview

-   **Position Level**: Entry-level/Intern (Penultimate year students)
-   **Department**: Product, Devices and Engineering
-   **Reporting Structure**: Reports to mentors and managers, works with delivery teams and fellow interns
-   **Location**: Hybrid (Albany, Auckland HQ + remote), on-site parking provided

### Tools & Platforms

-   **Development**: Modern IDEs, version control (Git), collaborative development tools
-   **Infrastructure**: AWS cloud platform, Linux environments
-   **Collaboration**: Agile tools (stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives), team brainstorming
-   **Monitoring**: Testing frameworks, automation tools, quality assurance systems

## Soft Skills & Behavioral Requirements

### Communication & Collaboration

-   Strong verbal and written interpersonal skills (essential requirement)
-   Ability to collaborate with team members in design, development, and testing
-   Participation in team brainstorming sessions and contribution to new ideas
-   Work effectively with mentors, delivery teams, and fellow interns

### Leadership & Initiative

-   Enthusiastic and collaborative approach to work
-   Positive attitude and willingness to take on challenging projects
-   Contribution to team initiatives and projects beyond core responsibilities
-   "Garage project" or portfolio showcasing personal initiative and passion

### Problem-Solving & Innovation

-   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills (explicitly required)
-   Love for learning and solving difficult problems within a team environment
-   Passion for working in a fast-paced tech environment
-   Ability to contribute to development of new ideas and features

## Experience Requirements

-   **Total Experience**: 12 months or fewer professional working experience in Technology business
-   **Relevant Experience**: Penultimate year of study in relevant field (Computer Science, Engineering, Software, Mechatronics, etc.)
-   **Education**: Engineering & Computer Science backgrounds preferred, open-minded approach to other relevant fields
-   **Portfolio/Projects**: "Garage project" or personal portfolio to demonstrate passion and skills

## Company Culture Indicators

-   **Values Alignment**: Collaboration, innovation, fun, diversity, respect, integrity
-   **Work Style**: Fast-paced tech environment, collaborative team approach, hybrid work model
-   **Growth Mindset**: Strong emphasis on learning and development, mentorship program, career pathway shaping
-   **Diversity & Inclusion**: Values diversity, team members from 35+ countries, open-minded approach to backgrounds

## Key Responsibilities Breakdown

1. **Core Development Work** (60-70% of time)
    - Collaborate with team members to design, develop, and test features
    - Work on bug fixes and enhancements to products
    - Learn and apply new technologies and development methodologies
2. **Agile Participation** (20-30% of time)
    - Participate in Agile development practices (stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives)
    - Engage in team brainstorming sessions and idea development
    - Contribute to team initiatives and projects
3. **Learning & Development** (10-20% of time)
    - Work with mentors on skill development and career pathway
    - Participate in onboarding and guidance from dedicated buddy
    - Engage in social events and networking with EROADers

## Red Flags & Concerns

-   **Application Timing**: Tight deadline (June 27, 2025) with Assessment Centre Day on August 6, 2025
-   **Eligibility Requirements**: Must be legally permitted to work in New Zealand and available full-time Nov 2025 - Feb 2026
-   **Experience Paradox**: Seeking students with minimal professional experience but expecting strong technical skills
-   **Competitive Process**: Assessment Centre Day suggests highly competitive selection process
-   **Geographic Limitation**: Role specifically based in Albany, Auckland with hybrid model
