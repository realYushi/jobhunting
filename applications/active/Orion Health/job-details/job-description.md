### About Orion Health

Orion Health is a leading healthcare technology company that has been reimagining healthcare delivery for over 30 years. With our recent acquisition by HEALWELL AI, we're positioned to accelerate innovation in AI-enhanced healthcare solutions while maintaining our leadership in healthcare interoperability. We manage healthcare data for 150+ million people across 70+ customer sites globally, making every line of code contribute to better patient outcomes worldwide.

### Role Purpose

Join our "community of start-ups" culture as a Software Engineer passionate about transforming healthcare through technology. You'll work on cutting-edge healthcare platforms that enable seamless data exchange, improve patient engagement, and provide actionable health insights through our unified healthcare platform combining Amadeus Digital Care Record, Virtuoso Digital Front Door, and Orchestral Health Intelligence Platform.
