# Orion Health - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Industry**: Healthcare Technology / Health Information Systems
**Founded**: 1993 (30+ years of experience)
**Headquarters**: Auckland, New Zealand
**Global Presence**: 17 offices across 11 countries worldwide
**Employees**: Global staff across multiple countries
**Focus**: 100% digital health solutions

## Mission & Vision

**Vision**: To reimagine the healthcare experience for all, ensuring every individual receives the perfect care for them.

**Mission**: 
- **Empower** people to take control of their healthcare
- **Reveal** insights from health data 
- **Unify** healthcare silos seamlessly and securely across the health ecosystem

## Business Model & Market Position

### Core Business
- Healthcare technology company creating digital tools to change healthcare delivery globally
- Unified Healthcare Platform combining multiple integrated solutions
- Focus on health data interoperability and population health management
- Managing data for 150+ million people across 70+ customer sites globally

### Market Leadership
- Leading provider of unified healthcare platforms
- Extensive experience in health information exchange and interoperability
- Strong presence in government and large healthcare organization implementations
- Global expansion across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Middle East

## Product Portfolio

### 1. **Amadeus Digital Care Record (DCR)**
- Integrated health record consolidating patient data across care settings
- Multidisciplinary approach to patient care
- Key components:
  - Clinical Portal
  - Medication Management
  - Care Pathways
  - Collaborative Worklists

### 2. **Virtuoso Digital Front Door (DFD)**
- Omnichannel consumer engagement platform
- End-to-end healthcare navigation and management
- Components:
  - Consumer Website
  - Patient Portal
  - Forms and digital intake

### 3. **Orchestral Health Intelligence Platform (HIP)**
- Data aggregation and analytics platform
- Journey from data to trusted insights
- Features:
  - Data Aggregation
  - Reporting & Analytics
  - Data Science & Machine Learning
  - Population health insights

### 4. **Additional Products**
- **Communicate**: Direct Secure Messaging (DirectTrust certified)
- **Clinical Workstation**: Healthcare provider tools
- **Hospital EPR**: Electronic patient records
