# Orion Health - Application Strategy

## Executive Summary

Orion Health represents an exceptional opportunity for software engineers passionate about healthcare transformation. As a recently acquired subsidiary of HEALWELL AI, the company is positioned for accelerated growth in AI-enhanced healthcare solutions while maintaining its 30-year legacy of healthcare interoperability innovation. The company's "community of start-ups" culture, global reach, and cutting-edge technology stack make it an ideal environment for technical professionals seeking meaningful impact in digital health.
