# Company Profile: Eagle Technology

## Basic Information

-   **Company Name**: Eagle Technology
-   **Industry**: Technology, specifically Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and IT Services
-   **Size**: The company has grown to include **120 staff** and is generally listed as having **100 - 500 employees**.
-   **Founded**: **1969**.
-   **Headquarters**: While a single headquarters isn't specified, Eagle Technology has offices in **Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch**.
-   **Website**: The sources imply the website domain is **eagle.co.nz**, as indicated by the emergency support email `emsupport@eagle.co.nz` and the general context of the web page excerpts.

## Mission & Values

Eagle Technology was founded with a vision to **bring excellence in IT products and services to the New Zealand market**. This vision is still committed to by the current Managing Director, Duane Eagle. The company engages with community groups to **further the use of technology in enabling conservation, education, and not-for-profit groups** that support communities. They are the official Esri distributor for New Zealand and the South Pacific, aiming to bring Geographic Information Systems (GIS) or location to the forefront of people's minds. They also support their customers during emergencies. Eagle Technology values **unique perspectives** and encourages thoughtful and effective communication. The company has evolved significantly with technological advancements.

## Products & Services

Eagle Technology offers a comprehensive range of GIS and IT solutions:

-   **Geographic Information Systems (GIS)**: They are the **official Esri distributor for New Zealand and the South Pacific**, primarily working with the **ArcGIS system**. GIS allows for visualising, questioning, analysing, and interpreting data to understand relationships, patterns, and trends, helping organisations make better decisions, especially regarding location.
-   **Emergency Management GIS**: They provide dedicated emergency support, capability building through practical workshops and customised training, and an Emergency Management Hub resource. GIS is integral to emergency management in New Zealand for decision-making before, during, and after events.
-   **GIS in Conservation**: They support **NZ GIS In Conservation (GiC)**, a charitable organisation fostering GIS use by volunteer conservation groups. They are also a stakeholder and supporter of **Predator Free NZ (PFNZ)**, which aims to eliminate introduced predators to protect native species.
-   **GIS in Education**: They support the integration of GIS in education, helping students explore their world, promoting critical thinking, and acquiring valuable skills for the twenty-first-century workforce. This includes programs for schools and higher education.
-   **IT Products & Services**: Beyond GIS, they offer other IT products and services.
-   **Professional Services**: These include **Consulting, Support, Training, and Technical Services**. They also provide **IT Service Management**.
-   **Solutions**: Specific solutions offered include **LocalMaps** and **FieldOps**.
-   **Resources**: They provide access to GIS Products, Imagery, and Content.

## Recent News & Achievements

-   **Trevor Eagle**, one of the founders, was posthumously awarded the **Flying Kiwi award in 2007** at the PricewaterhouseCoopers Hi-Tech Awards for his sustained and outstanding contribution to New Zealand’s Hi-Tech industries.
-   The **GIS Summer Intern Programme** applications for the November 2025 start were open, closing on 18 July 2025.

## Company Culture

Eagle Technology was founded as a **family company** and retains its family ownership and core values today. Employees generally describe a **good working environment** with endless opportunities to learn and resources for upskilling. There is support for learning new skills and flexibility in working location. Employees appreciate receiving different tasks, constantly learning, and improving their GIS knowledge. However, some feedback mentions that there can be **short timelines** to finish work, pay is lower compared to similar industry roles, and workloads can ramp up to very high levels. The GIS Summer Intern Programme is designed to offer outstanding professional and personal development opportunities with ongoing learning.

## Notable Leadership

-   **Trevor and Corallie Eagle**: **Founded Eagle Technology in 1969**. Trevor Eagle was a leader in the information technology industry, known for supporting science, education, community, and environmental organisations. Corallie Eagle became Chairman after Trevor's death in 2000, retaining the position until her death in 2021.
-   **Duane Eagle**: The current **Managing Director**, who continues the legacy started by his parents, Trevor and Corallie Eagle. He is committed to the company's founding vision and sees new opportunities as technologies like GIS and cloud services integrate. All seven of Trevor and Corallie's children have worked within the company at some point.
