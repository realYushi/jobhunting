# Culture Notes: Eagle Technology

## Work Environment

The work environment at Eagle Technology is often described as **good** and **supportive**. Employees typically experience **varied daily tasks**, with opportunities to work on client projects, including creating and completing projects with multiple ArcGIS applications, and conducting off-site demonstrations. Mid-level roles involve managing GIS software and its infrastructure, which includes tuning services, maintaining ETL tasks, installing/upgrading/patching software, reporting, troubleshooting, database maintenance, backup/restore, and managing cloud services related to client GIS.

Interns and graduates collaborate with staff members on projects and may also undertake self-led projects, which can be challenging but rewarding. There is a strong emphasis on **continual learning and improving GIS knowledge**, with resources provided for upskilling. The company fosters the development of technical and problem-solving skills, alongside enhanced **collaboration, communication, and presentation abilities**.

However, some employees note that there can be **short timelines** to finish work and that workloads can occasionally **ramp up to very high levels**. One graduate intern expressed a desire for more responsibilities, sometimes feeling as though they were "just filling in time with training". The company's offices are located in **Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch**. Eagle Technology also hires spatially enabled people from a diverse range of fields, contributing to a varied workforce.

## Company Values in Practice

Eagle Technology was founded in 1969 as a **family company** with the core vision of bringing excellence in IT products and services to the New Zealand market. This founding vision and the family ownership and values are retained today, with the Managing Director, Duane Eagle, continuing the legacy of his parents, Trevor and Corallie Eagle. All seven of the founders' children have worked within the company at some point.

The company actively demonstrates its commitment to **community engagement** by enabling conservation, education, and not-for-profit groups through the use of technology. This includes providing significant support to **Emergency Management GIS in New Zealand**, offering dedicated support, workshops, and customised training for capability building. They are a strong supporter of **NZ GIS In Conservation (GiC)**, a charitable organisation promoting GIS use by volunteer conservation groups, by providing software and support. Additionally, Eagle Technology is a stakeholder and supporter of **Predator Free NZ (PFNZ)**, a trust dedicated to eliminating introduced predators to protect native species.

In education, Eagle Technology actively supports the integration of GIS to help students explore their world, promoting critical thinking and valuable skills for the twenty-first-century workforce. The company values **unique perspectives** and explicitly encourages thoughtful and effective communication in its application processes. They seek candidates who possess a keen interest in GIS, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and excellent communication and teamwork abilities. The company's leadership is committed to seeing new opportunities and providing "whole care solutions to our customers" as technology evolves and integrates, particularly with GIS and cloud services.

## Employee Reviews & Insights

Eagle Technology holds an overall positive employer rating of **4.3**.
**Pros** frequently mentioned by employees include:

-   **Endless opportunities to learn**.
-   A **good working environment** with resources to upskill.
-   **Flexibility of working location**.
-   Support for learning new skills and upskilling.
-   Receiving diverse tasks that facilitate learning different skills.
-   A constant process of learning and improving GIS knowledge.

**Cons** or areas for improvement noted by employees include:

-   Sometimes experiencing **short timelines** to complete work.
-   Perception of being **paid lower** in comparison to similar roles within the industry.
-   Workloads that can **ramp up to very high levels** at times.
-   A sentiment from one graduate that they sometimes felt like they were "just filling in time with training" and desired more responsibilities.

## Benefits & Perks

Eagle Technology offers a range of benefits and perks, particularly highlighted for its GIS Summer Intern Programme. This programme provides a **paid internship** with a salary of NZD 51,000 per year, pro-rated for the internship period. Interns have access to **outstanding professional and personal development opportunities**, including ongoing learning opportunities both face-to-face and online. The company provides resources for general employee upskilling.

## Remote/Hybrid Policy

While general employee feedback mentions "Flexibility of working location" as a positive aspect of working at Eagle Technology, the GIS Summer Intern Programme specifically is designed to be an **on-site experience**. This is intended to "facilitate hands-on learning and team collaboration" at their Auckland, Wellington, or Christchurch offices.

## Growth Opportunities

The company strongly emphasises growth and learning for its employees. There are **"endless opportunities to learn"** and ample resources and support for **upskilling** and learning new skills. For those in the GIS Summer Intern Programme, opportunities include:

-   Discovering and exploring the latest geospatial information technology within the ArcGIS System.
-   Gaining **real-world work experience** in the geospatial (GIS) industry over a ten-week period.
-   Accessing **outstanding professional and personal development opportunities** with ongoing learning, both face-to-face and online.
-   Exposure to projects that build both technical and problem-solving skills.
-   Hands-on experience with core ArcGIS products and a deeper understanding of GIS applications in real-world business scenarios.
-   Enhancing collaboration, communication, and presentation skills.
-   Gaining insight into the spatial industry in New Zealand and potential career paths.
-   The structured graduate programme allows individuals to work on projects based on their passion after an intensive GIS training period.

Beyond specific programmes, Eagle Technology provides professional services, including consulting, support, training, and technical services. The company's history shows a continuous evolution with technological advances, indicating a dynamic environment where adaptation and new skill development are ongoing. They also offer practical emergency management workshops and customised training to build capability.
