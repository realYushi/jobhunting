# Culture & Work Environment: Kitea Health

## Work Environment
- **Remote/Hybrid/Office**: On-site in Parnell, Auckland (based on internship location)
- **Team Size**: Small, tight-knit team (~15-20 employees)
- **Work Hours**: Standard business hours, 10-week structured internship program
- **Location**: Modern office space in Parnell, Auckland central business district

## Company Culture
- **Values**: 
  - **Mission-Driven**: "Making a difference in the world of healthcare"
  - **Scientific Rigor**: Evidence-based approach grounded in university research
  - **Patient Impact**: Focus on improving real outcomes for chronic disease patients
  - **Innovation**: Solving "big, real world problems in health"
  - **Persistence**: "We refused to give up" mentality in facing deep tech challenges

- **Communication Style**: 
  - Professional yet approachable (CEO often seen "with flat white in hand")
  - Open door policy evidenced by CEO's willingness to mentor other entrepreneurs
  - Collaborative academic culture from university origins

- **Collaboration Approach**: 
  - Cross-functional teams (Quality, Data Science, Mechatronics, Software)
  - Research-industry partnership with University of Auckland
  - International clinical advisory group integration
  - Multi-disciplinary problem solving

## Learning & Development
- **Growth Opportunities**: 
  - Structured 10-week internship program with multiple disciplines
  - Direct exposure to medical device development lifecycle
  - FDA regulatory process experience
  - Clinical trial participation opportunities

- **Learning Culture**: 
  - University-industry collaboration provides research environment
  - Design controls and quality systems training
  - Mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs (Simon Malpas has 3 exits)
  - Access to clinical advisory expertise

- **Career Paths**: 
  - Multiple technical tracks (Quality/Regulatory, Data Science, Software, Mechatronics)
  - Early-stage company offers broad exposure and responsibility
  - Leadership team with strong industry connections

## Diversity & Inclusion
- **Leadership Diversity**: Female COO (Natalia Lopez) in senior leadership
- **International Perspective**: Team includes US and international advisors
- **Academic Integration**: University partnership brings diverse academic perspectives
- **Age Diversity**: Mix of experienced professionals and emerging talent through internship programs

## Employee Benefits
- **Internship Program**: 
  - 10-week paid summer internships
  - Hands-on experience in innovative medical technology
  - Real project ownership and impact
  - Professional development in regulated medical device environment

- **Professional Development**:
  - Exposure to international medical device industry
  - Clinical trial and regulatory experience
  - Technology transfer and commercialization knowledge
  - Networking with clinical and industry experts

## Team Dynamics & Leadership Style
- **CEO Leadership**: Simon Malpas described as having "loyalty, humility, and integrity"
- **Team Building**: "Teams that last" philosophy with people staying "for decades"
- **Mentorship Culture**: CEO regularly mentors other entrepreneurs and gives back to community
- **Scientific Background**: Leadership maintains academic positions while running company
- **Supportive Environment**: "Makes space for ideas" and "uplifts people"

## Innovation Environment
- **Risk Taking**: Comfortable with deep tech challenges and long development cycles
- **Research Culture**: Strong ties to University of Auckland and academic research
- **Clinical Focus**: Patient stories and outcomes drive decision making
- **Quality First**: Medical device standards embedded in company culture
- **Global Perspective**: FDA approval pathway and international market focus
