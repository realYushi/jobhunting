# Deloitte New Zealand — AI Engineer

**Location:** Auckland, New Zealand (On-site — Level 20, 1 Queen Street)
**Type:** Full-time
**Department:** Consulting | Technology | AI & Data (incl. GenAI)
**Compensation:** NZD 80,000 - NZD 140,000 / year
**Source captured:** 2026-05-13

## Eligibility

Must be legally entitled to work in New Zealand at time of application.

## Role

Hands-on, full-stack delivery of generative and agentic AI projects across multiple industries. Architect and deploy multi-agent workflows with tool use and function calling, tightly integrated with enterprise data platforms.

## A Week in the Life

- Engineering end-to-end generative and agentic AI solutions, from concept to production.
- Capture technical requirements with cross-functional Deloitte teams and architect/build/deploy in complex enterprise environments.
- Stand up LLMOps capabilities: automated evaluations, observability and tracing, offline/online testing, prompt versioning, safety redteaming, cost/latency optimisation.
- Produce models and pipelines using containerisation, CI/CD, IaC, cloud native services. Embed security, privacy, and Responsible AI controls.
- Implement retrieval and grounding strategies (chunking, hybrid search, metadata filtering) and feedback loops to improve reliability, quality, factuality.
- Partner with engagement leads to scope, estimate, and deliver work packages; contribute to accelerators and reference architectures.
- Continuous learning across new GenAI tools and frameworks.
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship, particularly to junior team members.

## Qualifications

- Experience as a Generative AI Engineer / Agentic AI Engineer / Technical Analyst / Consultant / Lead in a customer-facing role.
- Strong cloud-native engineering: CI/CD, DevOps / LLMOps, IaC.
- Business analysis and functional design skills.
- Track record architecting and delivering end-to-end generative and agentic AI on AWS, Azure, GCP, or OCI (certs beneficial).
- Hands-on with vector databases, embedding models, retrieval frameworks, evaluation techniques.
- LLM safety, governance, risk: prompt-injection defences, PII handling, content moderation, auditability.
- Observability and evaluation tools, performance profiling, cost management.
- DevOps best practices using Git; monorepo experience advantageous.
- Exceptional communication, collaboration, stakeholder management; mentorship.
- Genuine passion for generative AI and continuous learning.
