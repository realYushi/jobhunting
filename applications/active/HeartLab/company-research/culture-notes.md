# HeartLab Culture & Values Analysis

## Company Culture Overview

HeartLab demonstrates a mission-driven culture focused on meaningful healthcare impact, with strong emphasis on innovation, collaboration, and making a real difference in patient care outcomes.

## Core Values & Cultural Indicators

### 1. Mission-Driven Purpose
**Evidence from Research:**
- Clear mission to "revolutionise cardiac diagnosis" and address the "cardiovascular disease crisis"
- Focus on "creating positive change for cardiology teams"
- Emphasis on "ultimately benefiting patients" through their technology
- CEO Will Hewitt's background shows commitment to healthcare innovation

**Cultural Implication:** Employees are likely motivated by meaningful work that directly impacts patient outcomes and healthcare efficiency.

### 2. Innovation & Technology Excellence
**Evidence from Research:**
- Continuous platform improvement with "ever-improving platform" messaging
- AI partnerships with cutting-edge companies (Us2.ai, Ultromics)
- "Secure by Design principles" showing proactive security approach
- Browser-based, cloud-native architecture representing modern tech choices

**Cultural Implication:** Strong engineering culture that values technical excellence, modern architecture, and staying at the forefront of healthcare technology.

### 3. Customer-Centric Approach
**Evidence from Research:**
- Extensive customer testimonials prominently featured
- Direct quotes from customers about time savings and workflow improvements
- Personalized customer success stories (West Michigan Cardiology case study)
- Focus on "making clinicians' lives easier leading to better patient care"

**Cultural Implication:** Culture prioritizes customer success and real-world impact over just technical features.

### 4. Collaborative & Supportive Environment
**Evidence from Research:**
- Platform designed for "real-time collaboration" between healthcare teams
- Emphasis on "enhanced collaboration" and "remote work capabilities"
- Recent leadership expansion shows investment in team growth
- Inclusive pricing model (unlimited users) reflects collaborative values

**Cultural Implication:** Internal culture likely mirrors the collaborative tools they build, with emphasis on teamwork and mutual support.

### 5. Global Ambition with Local Roots
**Evidence from Research:**
- Strong New Zealand/Auckland base with global expansion (US, Australia)
- Active presence at international conferences (ACC.25 in Chicago)
- Hiring Head of Global Sales indicates serious international growth plans
- Maintaining NZ headquarters while expanding globally

**Cultural Implication:** Proud of NZ tech heritage while thinking globally, likely offering international exposure opportunities.

## Cultural Fit Indicators for Candidates

**Ideal Cultural Fit:**
- Passionate about healthcare technology and patient outcomes
- Enjoys collaborative, mission-driven work environments
- Interested in modern cloud technologies and AI
- Values technical excellence and security
- Excited about global impact and international expansion

**Red Flags:**

- Not interested in healthcare or patient impact
- Uncomfortable with rapid growth and changing responsibilities
- Prefers purely local/domestic focus over international exposure
