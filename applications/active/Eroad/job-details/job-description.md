At EROAD, we are passionate about making a positive impact on society through technology and innovation. We empower the transportation industry with reliable and accurate data to help improve safety, efficiency, and environmental sustainability.

EROAD’s Internship Programme in our Product, Devices and Engineering team is designed to provide you with hands on experience and give you invaluable commercial experience.

The Roles:

We are hiring for the below roles as part of our 2025-2026 Summer Internship Programme:

Software Engineer Interns

Firmware Engineer Interns

You will gain valuable experience in your desired field and will work on real-life projects alongside experienced team members. Working closely with mentors, delivery teams and fellow interns, you will be offered the opportunity to be part of a team that is committed to delivering excellence and creating a better world tomorrow.

You will have access to innovative technology, work on challenging projects, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in tech. Whether it is developing existing and new features, solving complex problems, or researching new ways of doing things, you will be empowered to contribute to our vision, purpose, and the betterment of society. You will be onboarded into the organisation and will have regular developmental conversations with mentors and managers.

Let’s talk about you!

You are ideally in your penultimate year of study in a relevant field. In the past we have had applicants from Engineering & Computer Science backgrounds, including but not limited to – Computer Systems, Software, Mechatronics, Engineering Science, Computer Science, and more. We are open minded!

You may also have some or all of the following:

What makes EROAD's Internship Programme special?

EROAD is a true Kiwi success story in the tech sector! Publicly listed in 2012, we are represented on the NZX and ASX and recently completed the largest Tech merger in New Zealand's history. We continue to grow rapidly, with over 400 EROADers based in NZ, Australia and the USA.

EROAD is committed to making your internship experience a great one. For example, you will be onboarded and guided by a dedicated buddy who will introduce you to all things EROAD and our customers. You will be treated like a full-time employee alongside your team members, participate in team ceremonies and work on projects that deliver real value to EROAD and our customers. You will receive 1:1 mentorship from your manager who will work with you to shape your career pathway, and you will enjoy participating in social events in the office and make connections with our amazing EROADers.

EROAD’s summer Internship Programme will run from November 2025 to February 2026, and many of our past internships have resulted in Graduate placements and long-term careers with EROAD! We ensure you have the tools, technology and training to do your best work, and hope to offer you an entry into the exciting world we create!
