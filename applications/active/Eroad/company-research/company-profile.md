## Products & Services

EROAD offers a range of solutions that leverage technology to enhance fleet and operational performance, increase efficiency, enhance productivity, and manage safety, compliance, and sustainability. These include:

-   **RUC Compliance**: Solutions for buying, displaying, and managing Road User Charges, including NZTA-approved options. EROAD is the **largest provider of RUC compliance in New Zealand**.
-   **Health & Safety**: Solutions designed to reduce speeding and accidents, and manage driver fatigue.
-   **Fleet Management**: Provides real-time decision-making, reporting, and operational management tools.
-   **Asset Tracking**: Helps protect assets from theft or loss, maximise ROI, and monitor location and usage.
-   **Hardware**: Offers a variety of 4G trackers, telematics devices, dashcams, and AI-powered devices.
-   **Video Telematics**: Solutions including dashcam and AI video to reduce accidents and protect drivers.
-   **Fleet Sustainability**: Aids in saving fuel, improving efficiency, and reducing emissions through fuel and sustainability solutions.
-   **GPS Vehicle Tracking**: Allows companies to track vehicles and improve fleet efficiency.

EROAD was the **first company in the world to implement a GNSS/cellular-based road charging solution across an entire country**.

## Recent News & Achievements

EROAD is listed on both the New Zealand Stock Exchange (NZX) and Australian Stock Exchange (ASX) under the stock symbol ERD. The company acquired Coretex in 2021, integrating its co-founders Dean Marris and Craig Marris into EROAD's executive team. This acquisition brought significant expertise in telematics and data analytics to EROAD.
