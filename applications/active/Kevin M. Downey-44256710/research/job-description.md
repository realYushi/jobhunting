About the job

(Variants: AI Developer • LLM / Agent Developer • Applied AI Developer • Generative AI Developer)

Another remote AI build opportunity from Kevin M. Downey…

Are you a skilled AI developer who wants to build real production systems for U.S. companies - full-time, long-term, from home? We work with established U.S. businesses that are putting AI into the way they run, and they need developers who can take an idea past the demo and into production where it actually works. This is full-time, long-term contract work with one of our U.S. clients - building agentic systems on top of today's best models. We work with strong developers across the world, and we're building a bench now so we can match you to the right client as roles open.




Because you'll work directly with U.S. clients - explaining what you build, training their teams on how to use it, and answering questions live - fluent, clear English (written and spoken) is essential, not optional. If you've built real AI systems, can speak to them, and can teach them, we want to meet you.




The Highlights

Full-Time, Long-Term Remote Contract Roles with U.S. Companies
Work From Home, Build Real Production AI - Not One-Off Gigs
Engaged as a Full-Time Independent Contractor, Paid in U.S. Dollars
Build on Top of the Best Models - Claude, GPT, Retrieval (RAG), Agents, and Integrations
Client-Facing Role - You'll Build It, Explain It, and Train Their Team to Use It
Long-Term Stability and a Real Seat on a U.S. Team
We Match You to the Right Client, Not Just the First Opening




What You'll Build

Day to day, you'll build and ship AI systems that real users and operations depend on:

LLM-Powered Applications and Agentic Workflows
Retrieval (RAG) Pipelines and Vector Database Work
Agent Frameworks, Tool Use, and Orchestration
Integrations That Wire AI Into a Real Product or Operation
Production-Ready Code the Client's Team Can Rely On After You Ship




The Requirements

We are not looking for people who can talk about AI. We are looking for people who have built it and can prove it. As part of your application, you'll be asked to walk us through the most complex AI system you've personally built - what it did, your specific role, the hardest technical decision you made, and how you'd verify it worked. That story matters more to us than any title on your resume.

A Track Record of AI Systems You Personally Built and Shipped to Production
Strong Hands-On Experience with LLM APIs, RAG, Vector Databases, and Agent Frameworks
Fluent, Clear English - Written and Spoken - You Will Talk with Clients Directly, Present Your Work, and Train Their Teams
Comfortable Being Client-Facing - Explaining Technical Work to Non-Technical People and Running Live Training Sessions
Availability to Work Full-Time, with Meaningful Overlap with U.S. Business Hours
A Reliable Computer, Secure High-Speed Internet, and a Quiet Place to Work
Clear Communication - You Can Explain What You Built and Why It Works




Admirable Traits

Independent, Technical, Results-Driven, Self-Motivated, Detail-Oriented, Dependable, Reliable, Strong Communicator




Our Clients

We work directly with U.S. companies whose growth and operations increasingly depend on AI that actually works in production. Because we represent many companies, this is not a single posting - it's how we meet talented developers and match them to the right long-term role as opportunities open.




The Recruiting Process

Once we receive your application, one of our recruiters will reach out to confirm your interest and that the role is a fit. From there, we represent you to the U.S. client whose work matches what you've actually built. Then it's a client interview, an offer, and a long-term seat building real AI from home.
