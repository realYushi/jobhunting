## Ko mātou tēnei | Who we are

Fundsorter is a social enterprise SaaS application that helps charities and community organisations across Aotearoa find and apply for grant funding. By combining a comprehensive database of funding opportunities with AI-powered tools and human support, Fundsorter reduces the time and stress of finding grants, writing grant applications and developing accountability reports, freeing up organisations to focus on their mission and impact.

Fundsorter is driven by a vision of a more equitable funding landscape, and a tech-enabled reduction in the funding burden. They want people working and volunteering in community organisations to spend more time on the mahi and less on resourcing.

Fundsorter is an early stage start-up focused on product improvements and growth, with a small, energetic and dedicated team.

## Mō tēnei tūranga mahi | About the role

This is a permanent part-time role, typically 20 to 35 hours per week. Hours are flexible by arrangement within New Zealand business hours. The role is remote anywhere in Aotearoa New Zealand, with optional Wellington co-working for local candidates and occasional travel for events and team meetings.

The developer will help build, ship and improve the Fundsorter platform, used by more than 1000 charities and community organisations across Aotearoa to find funding.

The team works extensively with AI tools as a core part of how they build software. The role includes hands-on work with customer-facing features, writing skills and prompts that drive AI-powered tooling, and automating behind-the-scenes work to keep the database up to date.

Because Fundsorter is a start-up, the role includes ownership, close collaboration, idea contribution, and pitching in beyond narrow tasks.
