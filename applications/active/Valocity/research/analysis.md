# Valocity Research Analysis - Intern Software Engineer Position

## Company Overview

### Core Business
Valocity is a leading property technology (PropTech) company headquartered in Auckland, New Zealand, specializing in transforming the property decisioning process for the finance and banking industry. The company operates within the 100-500 employee range and focuses on creating comprehensive property ecosystem solutions.

Valocity has established itself as an innovator in the intersection of property finance and technology, helping financial institutions streamline their property valuation and decision-making processes through advanced technological solutions.
