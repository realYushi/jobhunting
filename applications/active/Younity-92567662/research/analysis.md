# Mobile Developer (React Native) @ Younity

Source: https://nz.seek.com/job/92567662?type=standard&ref=search-standalone&origin=cardTitle#sol=b374da7e21358f3a00d3ced7bf85fd4d3fd8d6d2

We're looking for a Mobile Developer to take ownership of our mobile applications and help deliver a new production app. Working across iOS and Android using React Native, you'll be responsible for building, launching, and evolving the mobile experience. You'll work closely with designers and engineers, contribute to technical decisions, and play a key role in navigating the App Store and Google Play release process. This role suits someone who enjoys autonomy, takes ownership, and has experience delivering mobile apps into production.  Key Responsibilities  Develop and maintain mobile applications using React Native and TypeScript Take ownership of mobile application development and architecture Manage App Store and Google Play submission processes Implement secure authentication and mobile security best practices Build robust online/offline functionality and data synchronisation capabilities Leverage device-native features, including secure and encrypted storage Collaborate with designers to ensure designs are optimised for mobile platforms Work closely with engineers to ensure seamless integration with APIs and services Contribute to technical decision-making and mobile development standards  Skills & Experience Essential  Commercial experience developing mobile applications using React Native Strong TypeScript development experience Experience building and deploying applications to iOS and Android devices Proven experience submitting applications to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store Strong understanding of mobile development principles and platform-specific considerations Experience implementing authentication solutions, including OAuth and Single Sign-On (SSO)  Highly Desirable  Experience launching a new mobile application from concept through to production Knowledge of mobile security best practices Experience building offline-first mobile applications Understanding of mobile performance optimisation and app lifecycle management  What's in it for You?  Remote working  Own and shape the mobile capability from the ground up. Play a key role in launching a new mobile product. Work with modern technologies including React Native and TypeScript. Influence architecture, technical decisions, and best practices. Enjoy autonomy, flexibility, and the opportunity to make a real impact.  For further details please apply below and Alicia will be in touch. Please note we require you to based in NZ and full rights to work in NZ for this opportunity.
