# Figured Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Founded:** ~2014 (11-year-old company as of 2025)
**Founders:** Carl McDonald and David Marshall (farmers & accountants)
**Location:** New Zealand (HQ), with global operations across NZ, AU, UK, US, Canada
**Industry:** Agricultural Technology / Financial Management SaaS
**Size:** 75+ employees across 6 countries
**Mission:** Empowering farmers and agri accountants with AI-powered financial solutions

## Business Model & Market Position

### Core Platform
- Cloud-based SaaS platform for agricultural finance management
- Farm financial management software serving 30,000+ farming operations globally
- Deep integration with major accounting platforms (Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB)
- Real-time financial data and reporting for farming teams

### Target Markets
- **Primary Users:** Farmers (30,000+ operations)
- **Key Partners:** 500+ Partner Practices (accountants and advisors)
- **Geographic Reach:** New Zealand, Australia, UK, United States, Canada
- **Industries Served:** Dairy, Livestock, Crop farming, Mixed operations

### Product Portfolio
1. **Farm Planning & Budgeting:** Budget creation, scenario modeling up to 10 years
2. **Farm Operations:** Production tracking, livestock management, crop monitoring
3. **Farm Reporting:** Break-even analysis, profitability reports, cash flow forecasting
4. **Figured Insights:** Benchmarking and comparative analytics (NEW 2025)
5. **Commercial Manager:** Advanced features for larger operations
6. **Lending Solutions:** Financial products through partnerships (Heartland Bank)

## AI Integration Strategy

### Current AI Implementation
- **Auto Kill Sheet Processor:** AI-driven data extraction reducing 20-minute tasks to 1 minute
- **Meaningful AI Integration:** Not "AI sprinkles" but substantive implementation
- **Data Processing:** AI-powered benchmarking using existing farm data
- **Document Processing:** Automated invoice syncing and data extraction

### AI Vision
- Building "modern AI solution for the world's most essential industry"
- Focus on practical AI applications that solve real farming problems
- Data-driven insights and automated decision support
- Continuous innovation in agricultural AI technology
