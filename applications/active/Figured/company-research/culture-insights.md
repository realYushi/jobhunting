# Figured Culture Insights & Engineering Environment

## Company Values & Cultural Foundation

### Core Values (Behavioral Compass)
1. **Give a damn** - Genuine care for people, partners, and customers beyond just work
2. **Take ownership** - High sense of responsibility and impact-driven mindset
3. **Don't be a dick** - No personal agendas, politics, or egos; respect and inclusion focus
4. **Make bold decisions** - Decisions made by those closest to challenges and opportunities
5. **Work in the open** - Share early and often, harness diverse thinking from team members

### Cultural Characteristics
- **High collaboration** emphasis with transparency and flexibility
- **Impact and learning motivated** team members
- **Innovation at pace** through guiding principles rather than rigid processes
- **Celebrate risk-taking** - "sticking necks out" is encouraged
- **Take work seriously, but never ourselves** - balanced professional approach
