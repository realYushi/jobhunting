Serato, a New Zealand-based music software company, is known for its inclusive, music-loving, and innovative culture. Employees at Serato enjoy a collaborative environment where creativity is encouraged, and diverse perspectives are valued. They celebrate their passion for music through various activities and encourage employees to pursue their musical interests.
Here's a more detailed look at Serato's company culture:
Music-focused:
Serato is deeply embedded in music culture. Many employees are musicians or DJs themselves, and the company actively promotes music creation and appreciation within the workplace.
Inclusive and diverse:
Serato values diversity and inclusion, sponsoring women in tech events and striving to create a welcoming environment for all.
Collaborative and innovative:
Serato fosters a culture where experimentation is encouraged, and great ideas can come from anywhere.
Mature design culture:
With Human Centered Design at the core, they focus on creating intuitive tools for DJs and producers.
Strong work-life balance:
Glassdoor reviews indicate that Serato offers a good work-life balance, with flexible hours and a supportive environment.
Positive employee experience:
Employee reviews on Glassdoor show a high level of satisfaction with Serato's culture and working environment.
