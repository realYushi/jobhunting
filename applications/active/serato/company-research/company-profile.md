Serato is a New Zealand-based company founded in 1998 by Steve West and AJ Bertenshaw, specializing in music software for DJs and producers. They are known for their flagship product, Serato DJ, a digital music mixing software, and have a global user base of millions. Recently, a majority stake in Serato was acquired by the Canadian technology holding company, Tiny.
Here's a more detailed summary:
Founding and Location:
Serato was established in Auckland, New Zealand, in 1998.
Core Business:
The company develops and provides audio software, including Serato DJ, which is used for mixing digital music, and other tools for music production.
Global Reach:
Serato's software is used by a vast community of DJs, producers, engineers, and musicians across 190 countries.
Notable Users:
Many prominent artists, including DJ Snake, Timbaland, and Disclosure, have used Serato products.
Recent Acquisition:
A majority stake in Serato was recently acquired by Tiny, a Canadian technology holding company, in a deal that valued the company at $175 million.
Financial Highlights:
Serato has reported strong financial performance, with annualized revenue of $42.4 million, a 62% recurring revenue stream, and a 35% compound annual growth rate in paid subscribers over the past five years, according to BC Technology.
Future:
While acquired by Tiny, Serato will continue to operate as a standalone brand, maintaining its partnerships within the DJ hardware and software industry, reports BusinessDesk.
