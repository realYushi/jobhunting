About the job

Te whiwhinga mahi | The opportunity

We are looking for a Software Engineer to play a pivotal part in the future of our growing Software Engineering Centre of Excellence and support the business. Our Digital Development & Innovation team has 35 diverse, unique, and passionate individuals who have a strong identity and an ever-growing role in Beca’s purpose to ‘make everyday better’.

Our team loves to work collaboratively and enjoys partnering with other parts of our business on products/projects and for social events. Above all we enjoy continuous improvement, nurturing creativity, and respect everyone’s individual input.

We invite applicants from a range of backgrounds with the following experience, skills, and attributes:


Experience as a full stack developer with cloud-based software products, web development, systems integration using APIs, distributed systems, software security and storage systems
Enterprise experience across .NET, C#, Azure, Blazor, React, TypeScript, JavaScript, SQL, Azure DevOps, GitHub
Experience using cloud computing services, specifically Microsoft Azure, to provide software solutions with high availability requirements
Experience collaborating with Software Engineers and stakeholders leveraging your experience to drive innovative behaviours
AI augmented development practices (Claude Code and GitHub Copilot specifically)
Great communication skills with the ability to build meaningful and collaborative relationships across the Digital team


In addition, knowledge and experience across the following is not critical, however would be beneficial:


Agile development background using Scrum or other Agile methodologies 
Microsoft Dataverse, Fabric, Logic Apps, Data Factory, Power Apps
Experience with other programming languages such as Python


If you are interested in working in a collaborative high-performance team focused on innovation, quality and client centered design then keep reading.

Ko wai koe | About you

You enjoy working across different projects with a range of problems to solve, developing and delivering solutions and new Digital offerings. You enjoy working with a motivated team of Software Engineers and multi-disciplinary Engineers that combine technical ‘know how’ with enabling software and technologies. You are driven to continuously grow and improve your technical skill set as well as your soft skill set.
