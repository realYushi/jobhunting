# Digital Intern - Software Engineering | Air New Zealand

## Job Description
Air New Zealand is on a fast track to becoming the world's most digital airline, and we want you to be part of our Digital team at the forefront of tech innovation, where you will help create and enhance digital experiences that connect millions of travellers every year.

At Air New Zealand, you will work for an iconic brand at the cutting edge of technology and help the airline shape the future faster. You will be working in our experienced teams, fully utilising your degree and gaining valuable experience to start your career.

**Our Team - Software Engineering**

As an intern in our Software Engineering team, you'll work on full-stack software development and/or test automation within cross-functional squads. You'll contribute to Java microservices, front-end web development, mobile development, and test automation frameworks - supporting our mission to deliver innovative digital experiences for millions of travelers.

- Collaborate in cross-functional squads using agile methodologies
- Work on meaningful projects that impact the lives of millions of Kiwis
