# Air New Zealand - Culture Notes

## Company Values (Te Hononga)

### Four Core Values with Māori Integration
1. **Welcome as a Friend (Ko au ko koe, ko koe ko au)**
   - Working as one, building mana of others, having hui with right people
   - Everyone part of whānau (family) - collaborative software engineering teams
   - Cross-functional squad integration and mutual support in development

2. **Be Yourself (He toa takitini)**
   - Deeply valuing authenticity and individual technical contribution
   - Open transparent kōrero (conversation), confident idea sharing in code reviews
   - Psychological safety to express technical ideas and propose innovative solutions

3. **Can Do (Whāia te iti kahurangi)**
   - Constantly curious about new technologies, safety-first in software development
   - Excellence in coding basics, thinking big while starting small, acting fast
   - Proactive problem-solving and continuous technical improvement

4. **Share your Aotearoa (Ko Aotearoa e ngunguru nei)**
   - Showing manaaki in code quality, embracing Tiaki for sustainable technology
   - Deep connection to New Zealand identity and building technology for Kiwis
   - Sustainable coding practices and community-focused software solutions
