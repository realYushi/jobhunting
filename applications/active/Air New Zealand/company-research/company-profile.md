# Company Profile: Air New Zealand

## Company Overview
**Air New Zealand** is New Zealand's national airline and flag carrier, positioning itself as an iconic global brand with a mission to become "the world's most digital airline." The company operates extensive domestic and international networks, connecting New Zealanders to each other and New Zealand to the world.

## Key Information
- **Industry**: Aviation/Airline
- **Position**: Digital Intern - Software Engineering
- **Company Size**: 12,000+ Air New Zealanders worldwide, 5,000+ uniformed employees
- **Founded**: 1940 (as Tasman Empire Airways Limited)
- **Headquarters**: 185 Fanshawe Street, Auckland, New Zealand (Digital teams location)
- **Stock Exchange**: Listed on NZX (AIR) with Baa1 Moody's credit rating
- **CEO**: Greg Foran (former Walmart US CEO, since February 2020)
- **Chief Digital Officer**: Nikhil Ravishankar (since September 2021)
