# Company Culture: Air New Zealand

## Core Values & Cultural Foundation

Air New Zealand's values have been integral to the company for over a decade, forming the foundation of their culture. All employees truly embrace and live these values in their daily work, from interactions with colleagues to customer and community care.

### The Four Core Values

#### 1. Welcome as a Friend (Ko au ko koe, ko koe ko au)
- **Meaning**: Working as one, building the mana of others, having hui with the right people
- **Daily Practice**: Everyone is part of the whānau (family)
- **Cultural Expression**: Strong emphasis on collective success and mutual support
- **Workplace Impact**: Collaborative environment where team members actively support each other's growth

#### 2. Be Yourself (He toa takitini)
- **Meaning**: Deeply valuing authenticity and individual contribution
- **Daily Practice**: Open and transparent kōrero (conversation), confident idea sharing, active listening
- **Cultural Expression**: Celebrating diversity and encouraging unique perspectives
- **Workplace Impact**: Psychological safety to express ideas and be genuine

#### 3. Can Do (Whāia te iti kahurangi)
- **Meaning**: Constantly curious, safety-first mindset, excellence in basics, thinking big while starting small
- **Daily Practice**: Acting fast, taking pride in fundamentals, endless curiosity
- **Cultural Expression**: Innovation mindset combined with operational excellence
- **Workplace Impact**: Proactive problem-solving and continuous improvement culture

#### 4. Share your Aotearoa (Ko Aotearoa e ngunguru nei)
- **Meaning**: Showing manaaki in all actions, embracing Tiaki for wise resource use, passion for Aotearoa
- **Daily Practice**: Care for others, environmental stewardship, cultural pride
- **Cultural Expression**: Deep connection to New Zealand identity and values
- **Workplace Impact**: Sustainable practices and community-focused decision making
