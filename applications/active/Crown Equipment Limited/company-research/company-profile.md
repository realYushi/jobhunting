Company Profile: Crown Forklifts NZ

Basic Information
• Company Name: Crown Forklifts NZ
• Industry: Material Handling Equipment / Forklift Manufacturing
• Size: One of the top five largest lift truck manufacturers in the world; operates 24 manufacturing facilities globally
• Founded: 1945 (company origins), entered material handling equipment industry in 1960
• Headquarters: New Bremen, Ohio, USA
• Website: https://www.crown.com/en-nz/about-us.html

Mission & Values

Crown is dedicated to innovation, quality, and human-centered design in material handling equipment. The company focuses on improving productivity, comfort, and safety for operators, and is committed to providing superior value to customers through reliable products and responsive support.

Business Model

Crown designs, manufactures, distributes, services, and supports a wide range of material handling products, from hand pallet trucks to high-lifting turret trucks. The company controls up to 85% of its lift truck component manufacturing, ensuring quality and integration. Sales and service are delivered through a global network of Crown-owned and independent dealers, with factory-trained personnel.

Recent News & Achievements
• Crown has earned more than 80 prominent design awards for its products.
• The company continues to be recognized for innovation in the material handling industry.
• Crown’s QuickPick Remote technology and InfoLink fleet management system are examples of recent technological advancements.

Key Technologies
• InfoLink: Wireless operator and fleet management solution providing real-time business metrics.
• QuickPick Remote: Order-picking technology that increases productivity and reduces operator fatigue by minimizing the number of steps required during order selection.
• Automation & Fleet Management: Crown integrates advanced automation and fleet management technologies directly into its product design.

Competitive Advantages
• High degree of vertical integration (designs and manufactures up to 85% of components in-house)
• Strong focus on operator ergonomics, safety, and productivity
• Global presence with extensive sales, service, and support network
• Family-led company with a long history of innovation and design excellence
• Recognized for award-winning product design and technological leadership
