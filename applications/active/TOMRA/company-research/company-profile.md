# TOMRA Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Company Name**: TOMRA Systems ASA  
**Division Focus**: TOMRA Food  
**Industry**: Sensor-based sorting and food processing technology  
**Founded**: 1972 (50+ years in business)  
**Headquarters**: Asker, Norway  
**Global Presence**: Multinational organization with operations worldwide  

## Business Model & Market Position

### Core Business
- **Primary Focus**: Sensor-based sorting, peeling and integrated post-harvest solutions for food industries
- **Technology Leadership**: Leading provider of advanced sorting technology using sensors, AI, and machine learning
- **Market Position**: Global leader in food sorting and processing automation
- **Customer Base**: Food processors, agricultural producers, and food manufacturers worldwide

### Key Technologies
- **Sensor Technology**: Advanced optical sorting using cameras, lasers, and near-infrared sensors
- **AI & Machine Learning**: Intelligent sorting algorithms for food quality assessment
- **Automation Systems**: Integrated post-harvest solutions for food processing
- **Data Analytics**: Performance monitoring and optimization systems
