## Mission Statement
"Enable farmers to run the most productive and sustainable farms"

## Business Model & Core Product

### The Halter System
Halter provides the leading operating system for pasture-based farming, consisting of:

1. **Smart Solar-Powered Collars** - Lightweight collars that transmit animal data and respond to audio/vibration cues
2. **Transmission Towers** - Send data between collars and the app (no cell coverage required)
3. **Mobile & Desktop App** - Easy-to-use interface for remote farm management

### Core Capabilities
- **Virtual Fencing & Shifting:** Move and manage herds remotely, anywhere, anytime
- **Pasture Management:** Real-time data for optimal grazing and pasture planning
- **Animal Health & Mating:** Automatic heat detection and behavior monitoring (dairy only)
- **Data Analytics:** Comprehensive insights combining animal and pasture data
