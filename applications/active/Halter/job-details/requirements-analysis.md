## Context: Agricultural IoT Challenges

### Unique Technical Challenges
- **Environmental Durability**: Devices must withstand harsh outdoor conditions, weather extremes
- **Power Constraints**: Solar-powered systems with battery backup for extended operation
- **Connectivity**: Reliable communication in remote agricultural areas with poor cellular coverage
- **Real-time Requirements**: Immediate response to animal behavior for effective virtual fencing
- **Scale**: Managing thousands of devices in live production fleet
- **Safety**: Animal welfare considerations requiring robust, fail-safe systems

### Implied Technical Requirements
- Understanding of low-power design principles
- Experience with wireless communication in challenging environments
- Knowledge of GPS/GNSS systems for location tracking
- Familiarity with sensor integration (accelerometers, GPS, etc.)
- Understanding of edge computing for real-time decision making

## Red Flags & Concerns

- **High Responsibility**: Working on production systems that farmers "rely on 24/7" - requires careful, reliable code
- **Complex Domain**: Agricultural IoT combines hardware, firmware, connectivity, and animal behavior
- **Fast-paced Environment**: May be challenging for interns to keep up with startup pace
- **Office-first Culture**: Less remote work flexibility compared to other tech companies
- **Limited Guidance**: Balance of "self-directed learning" suggests need for independence

## Success Indicators

- Demonstrated ability to work independently while knowing when to seek help
- Strong problem-solving skills with evidence through projects or academic work
- Team collaboration experience, ideally in technical projects
- Genuine interest in agricultural technology or IoT systems
- Resilience and adaptability for startup environment challenges
